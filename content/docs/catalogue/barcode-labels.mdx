Print ready-to-stick price labels with a scannable barcode for any variant. Labels encode the variant's **Barcode** when it has one, and fall back to the **SKU** when it doesn't. Either way, the printed label scans at the [POS](/docs/orders/creating-orders) or with a dedicated scanner (see [Barcode scanning](/docs/orders/pos-barcode-scanning)).

## Printing Barcode Labels

1. Open the product details and find the variant (the variants table, or the details panel for single-variant products).
2. Click **Print labels**.
3. Choose the **Number of labels**. One A4 sheet fits **21 labels** (3 columns × 7 rows), and the dialog shows how many sheets will print.
4. Click **Print labels** in the dialog. Your browser's print dialog opens with the sheet. Print it, or choose **Save as PDF**.

### What's on each label

- The **product name** and **variant name**
- The **selling price**
- A **scannable barcode** of the variant's barcode (or its SKU when no barcode is set), readable by any barcode reader, including the one you use at the POS

Labels use the same barcode types real retailers use. Standard retail numbers (EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8) print in their native format, exactly like the barcodes on packaged goods. Anything else, such as a text SKU, prints as Code 128.

### Printing tips

- The layout matches common **A4 sticker sheets** (63.5 × 38.1 mm labels, 21 per sheet). You can also print on plain paper and cut along the dashed guides.
- Scan a printed label at the POS to add that exact variant to a sale instantly.

<Callout title="Note">
If nothing opens when you click **Print labels**, allow popups for the portal in your browser and try again. Code 128 barcodes need **Latin** letters, numbers, and dashes. Arabic letters and other non-Latin characters cannot be encoded. The easiest fix: open the variant, press the **Generate barcode** wand on the **Barcode** field to get a standard retail number, save, and print again.
</Callout>
