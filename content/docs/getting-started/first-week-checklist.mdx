---
title: First-Week Checklist
description: "A day-by-day plan for your first week on Cashvio: store setup, catalogue, first sales, and going live online."
---

Use this checklist after [Quick Setup](/docs/getting-started/onboarding). Each day builds on the last so you are selling confidently by the end of the week.

## Day 1: Account and store

1. [Sign up or log in](/docs/getting-started/signing-up).
2. Open **Settings > Stores** and confirm name, address, phone, and currency.
3. Enable [portal payment methods](/docs/stores/portal-payment-methods) and [fulfillment methods](/docs/stores/fulfillment-methods).
4. Walk the [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) so you know where Needs Attention and quick actions live.

## Day 2: Catalogue

1. Create [categories](/docs/catalogue/categories) that match how you shelve or sell.
2. Add [options](/docs/catalogue/options) if you sell sizes, colors, or other variants.
3. [Create your first products](/docs/catalogue/create-product), or [import from a file](/docs/catalogue/importing-products) if you already have a list.
4. Set cost prices so [profit reports](/docs/reports/profit-report) stay accurate later.

## Day 3: Stock and first sale

1. If you track inventory, set levels and [reorder points](/docs/inventory/low-stock-alerts).
2. Complete [your first sale](/docs/getting-started/your-first-sale) on **New Sale**.
3. Open the order in [Manage Orders](/docs/orders/managing-orders) and try print or share receipt.

## Day 4: Customers and team

1. Add a few [customers](/docs/customers/managing-customers) you sell to often.
2. Invite a cashier or manager under [Users](/docs/team/users) with the right [role](/docs/team/roles-permissions).
3. Practice attaching a customer on the next sale.

## Day 5: Online store (optional)

1. Follow [Go live online](/docs/getting-started/go-live-online).
2. Add hero images and contact channels under [storefront appearance](/docs/online-store/storefront-appearance) and [WhatsApp](/docs/online-store/social-whatsapp-button).
3. Place a test order from your phone and [track it](/docs/online-store/order-tracking).

## Day 6 to 7: Grow habits

1. Create one [coupon](/docs/marketing/coupons) for a real offer.
2. Check [sales overview](/docs/reports/sales-overview-report) for the week.
3. Turn on [sales digest notifications](/docs/settings/sales-digest-notifications) if you want an evening summary.

<Callout title="Tip">
Skip days that do not apply (for example, skip online store if you only sell in person). Come back when you are ready.
</Callout>
