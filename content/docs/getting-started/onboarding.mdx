## Your First Steps

When you sign up, Cashvio **automatically creates your first store** using your business details. You can start working right away — this guide walks you through the recommended first steps to get from a fresh account to your first sale.

<Callout title="Tip">
The Dashboard helps you along the way: a banner at the top guides you through important next steps (like putting your online store live), and the **Needs Attention** list shows anything that requires action, such as pending orders or low stock.
</Callout>

## 1. Review Your Store

Your first store is created during signup. Review its details before selling:

- Go to **Settings > Stores** and open your store.
- Check the store name, address, currency, and tax settings.
- Configure [payment methods](/docs/stores/payment-methods) and [fulfillment methods](/docs/stores/fulfillment-methods).

See [Creating a Store](/docs/stores/creating-store) for full details.

## 2. Add Your First Category

Categories organize your products (for example: "Electronics", "Clothing", "Food").

- Go to **Catalogue > Categories**.
- Click **Add Category**, enter a name, and save.

See [Categories](/docs/catalogue/categories) for detailed instructions.

## 3. Add Your First Product

Products are what you sell. You need at least one product to make a sale.

- Go to **Catalogue > Products**.
- Click **Add Product**, fill in the name, price, and category, and save.

See [Products](/docs/catalogue/products) for detailed instructions.

## 4. Make Your First Sale

Once you have a product, create a test order to see how the sales flow works:

- Click the **New Sale** button (always visible in the sidebar), or use the **New Sale** quick action on the Dashboard.
- Tap a product to add it to the cart, then take payment.

See [Creating Orders](/docs/orders/creating-orders) for the full walkthrough.

## 5. Put Your Online Store Live (Optional)

If you want to sell online:

- Go to **Online Store** in the sidebar.
- Configure your subdomain, hero images, and settings.

See [Online Storefront Overview](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview) for details.

<Callout title="Recommendation">
Complete these steps in order — it only takes a few minutes and helps you understand how the main features of Cashvio work together.
</Callout>
