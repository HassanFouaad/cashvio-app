Cashvio includes a simple **Invite a shop owner** share sheet on the Dashboard. Use it to send a personal signup link over WhatsApp (or copy the link) so another merchant can open a free account.

There are **no rewards or credits** for invites yet. We measure which links lead to signups first, then decide on incentives later.

## Where to find it

1. Open the **Dashboard** after you sign in.
2. In **Quick Actions**, tap **Invite a shop owner**.
3. A share sheet opens with your invite link.

## What the link does

Your invite link opens the Cashvio register page with tracking parameters:

- `utm_source=tenant_referral`
- `utm_medium=whatsapp`
- `utm_campaign=invite_shop_owner`
- `ref` set to your business id

When someone signs up from that link, first-touch attribution is stored in analytics so we can see which invites convert.

## Share options

| Action | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Invite via WhatsApp** | Opens the native share sheet when available, otherwise WhatsApp with a ready message and your link |
| **Copy invite link** | Copies the full URL so you can paste it in SMS, chat, or email |

## Tips

- Share with shop owners you know (friends, neighboring stores, suppliers who also sell).
- Prefer WhatsApp in Egypt. Most merchants already use it daily.
- Your customers do not need this link. Customer receipts use a different digital-receipt page.
