## What is the AI Assistant?

The **AI Assistant** is a chat panel inside the merchant portal. Ask questions in everyday language and get answers pulled from your live store data. When the assistant needs to change something (transfer stock, adjust inventory, update fulfillment, or create a refund), it shows an approval card and waits for you to tap **Approve** or **Deny**.

Open it from the sidebar (**Assistant**) or the Dashboard quick action. On mobile, **Assistant** also appears in the bottom navigation.

<Callout title="Plan and permissions">
The AI Assistant requires the **AI Assistant** plan feature. Analytics-style answers (sales overview, profit, top products, slow movers, inventory valuation) also require the **Analytics** module and order-view permission. Your role must allow the underlying action before the assistant can run a tool.
</Callout>

## Starting a conversation

1. Open **Assistant** from the sidebar.
2. Tap **New conversation** or pick a thread from the list.
3. Type a question or tap one of the suggested prompts on the welcome screen, for example:
   - List products that are low or out of stock
   - How are sales today?
   - Show my most recent orders
   - What are my top-selling products this month?
   - What is my inventory worth at cost and retail?
   - Which products have not sold in the last 60 days?

The assistant streams its reply in real time. While it works, you may see a **Thinking** indicator or a short note that a tool is running.

## What you can ask about

| Topic | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Products & stock** | Search products, read stock levels, list low-stock and out-of-stock items with names |
| **Orders** | Search recent orders, open order details |
| **Customers** | Search customers, open a customer profile |
| **Dashboard** | Today's key metrics for your store |
| **Analytics** | Sales overview, profit, top products, slow movers, inventory valuation (when Analytics is enabled) |

The assistant only sees data your account can access. If you manage multiple stores, inventory tools use the store selected in your session.

## Monthly business snapshot

The suggested **Monthly business snapshot** reviews the previous completed calendar month. For example, when you open it on October 1, the report covers September 1 through September 30 and labels the report **September**.

This keeps the snapshot complete and avoids mixing a few days from the new month with the previous month's results. To review the current month instead, ask for **this month to date**. The assistant will report from the first day of the current month through today.

<Callout title="Check the reported period">
The report heading and date range come from the same reporting period. Future dates are never included in sales totals.
</Callout>

## Approving store actions

Mutating tools never run silently. The assistant posts a **confirmation card** with a plain summary of what will happen. Tap **Approve** to execute or **Deny** to cancel. If you wait too long, the request expires and you can ask the assistant to prepare it again.

Examples of actions that require approval:

- Transfer stock between stores
- Adjust inventory quantities
- Update order fulfillment status
- Create a refund

Read-only lookups (search, details, analytics summaries) do not need approval.

## Structured input forms

When the assistant needs missing details before it can continue (for example refund amount, refund method, or an optional reason), it shows a compact **input card** in the thread instead of asking the same questions only in prose.

- Answer with suggested choices or use **Other** for a custom text or number when offered.
- Optional fields are marked and may be left blank.
- Tap **Continue** to submit. The assistant validates your answers and resumes the task.
- You can still type a normal message in the composer. That message counts as your answer and disables the earlier card.
- Submitting the form only supplies parameters. Mutations such as refunds still require a separate **Approve** step on the confirmation card.

## Daily usage limit

Your plan includes a daily **token** budget for assistant messages. A progress strip above the message box shows how much remains. When the limit is reached:

- You cannot send new messages until the limit resets
- **New conversation** is disabled
- Suggested prompts and archive actions are disabled until reset

The strip shows when usage resets (midnight in your tenant timezone).

## Managing conversations

- **Search**: when you have many threads, use the search box in the sidebar.
- **Archive**: remove a conversation from your list. Messages stay on record; archiving only hides the thread from the sidebar.
- **Copy**: copy any assistant or user message from the thread menu.

## Tips for better answers

- Name the store or product when you manage more than one location or have similar item names.
- For analytics, mention the date range you care about (today, this week, last 30 days).
- If the assistant asks you to pick between similar orders or products, reply with the number or name it shows.
