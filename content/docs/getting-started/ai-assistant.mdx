## What is the AI Assistant?

The **AI Assistant** is a chat panel inside the merchant portal. Ask questions in everyday language and get answers pulled from your live store data. When the assistant needs to change something (transfer stock, adjust inventory, update fulfillment, or create a refund), it shows an approval card and waits for you to tap **Approve** or **Deny**.

Open it from the sidebar (**Assistant**) or the Dashboard quick action. On mobile, **Assistant** also appears in the bottom navigation.

<Callout title="Plan and permissions">
The AI Assistant requires the **AI Assistant** plan feature. Analytics-style answers (sales overview, profit, top products, slow movers, inventory valuation) also require the **Analytics** module and order-view permission. Your role must allow the underlying action before the assistant can run a tool.
</Callout>

## Starting a conversation

1. Open **Assistant** from the sidebar (or bottom navigation on mobile).
2. **Switch threads:**
   - On desktop (wide screen), your conversation list stays docked on the left. Tap any thread to open it. Use the collapse control at the top of the list if you want more room for the chat.
   - On tablet and phone, tap **Conversations** in the header to open the list as a drawer, then pick a thread.
3. Tap **New conversation** (at the top of the conversation list, or last in the header) to start fresh, or choose a suggested prompt on the welcome screen, for example:
   - List products that are low or out of stock
   - How are sales today?
   - Show my most recent orders
   - What are my top-selling products this month?
   - What is my inventory worth at cost and retail?
   - Which products have not sold in the last 60 days?

Suggested prompts respect your plan and permissions. Analytics-style chips appear only when the **Analytics** module and order-view permission are enabled. Recently used prompts move to the front of the grid on your next visit.

The assistant streams its reply in real time. While it works, you may see a **Thinking** indicator or a short note that a tool is running. You can keep typing your next question in the composer during a reply; press **Enter** only after the current reply finishes. A message can be up to 8,000 characters; a counter appears near the limit.

- **Stop**: press **Stop** (or **Escape**) to end a reply. The assistant stops within a moment and keeps what it already wrote, marked as stopped.
- **Connection drops**: if your connection drops or you reload the page, the assistant keeps working on the server. The thread reconnects on its own and shows the finished answer. If it still cannot reconnect after a few tries, it shows **Reconnect** so you can try again when your connection is back. A dropped connection never cancels your request.
- **Drafts**: unsent drafts are saved per conversation, so switching threads and coming back keeps your text. Drafts belong to your account on this browser and are erased when you sign out, so the next person who signs in on the same device cannot see them. If your first message in a new conversation is not accepted, its text stays in the composer so you can try again.

To **archive** the open conversation, open **Other actions** in the header and choose **Archive conversation**. You can also archive from the row menu on any thread in the list.

## What you can ask about

| Topic | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Products & stock** | Search products, read stock levels, list low-stock and out-of-stock items with names |
| **Orders** | Search recent orders, open order details |
| **Customers** | Search customers, open a customer profile |
| **Dashboard** | Today's key metrics for your store |
| **Analytics** | Sales overview, profit, top products, slow movers, inventory valuation (when Analytics is enabled) |

The assistant only sees data your account can access. If you manage multiple stores, inventory tools use the store selected in your session.

- **Which stores an answer covers**: analytics answers say which store they cover. Ask for "all stores" to get figures across every store, if your role can see all of them; otherwise the assistant says it cannot. Stores that sell in different currencies are never added into one total.
- **Profit with missing costs**: if some sold products have no purchase cost, the assistant says the costs are missing and does not show a profit or margin as if those items cost nothing. Add the purchase cost to those products for a complete figure.
- **Restocking**: products at or below their reorder point are always included in restock suggestions, even when they sold little recently.
- **Searching**: a search with several words, such as "blue shirt", also finds products whose names contain those words in a different order.

## Monthly business snapshot

The suggested **Monthly business snapshot** reviews the previous completed calendar month. For example, when you open it on October 1, the report covers September 1 through September 30 and labels the report **September**.

This keeps the snapshot complete and avoids mixing a few days from the new month with the previous month's results. To review the current month instead, ask for **this month to date**. The assistant will report from the first day of the current month through today.

<Callout title="Check the reported period">
The report heading and date range come from the same reporting period. Future dates are never included in sales totals.
</Callout>

## Approving store actions

Mutating tools never run silently. The assistant posts a **confirmation card** with a plain summary of what will happen. When the backend includes an expiry time, the card shows the deadline and a live countdown. Tap **Approve** to execute or **Deny** to cancel. If you wait too long, the request expires in place and you can ask the assistant to prepare it again.

Examples of actions that require approval:

- Transfer stock between stores
- Adjust inventory quantities
- Update order fulfillment status
- Create a refund
- Save a purchase order draft

Read-only lookups (search, details, analytics summaries) do not need approval.

<Callout title="The approval matches what you saw">
If the record changes between the preview and your approval (for example someone already added that customer, or the refundable amount changed), the action does not run. The card says the preview no longer matches; ask the assistant to prepare it again. Approving the same card twice never repeats the action.
</Callout>

After you approve, the **Action result** card shows what actually happened:

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Created** / **Updated** | The customer was added, or the existing customer was updated. |
| **Refund requested** | The refund was saved. Cash and store-credit refunds usually show **Completed** right away. Card or online refunds show **Pending with the payment provider** until the provider confirms; use **Check status** to refresh. If the status changed since the action ran, the card also shows **At execution** with the earlier status. |
| **Purchase draft saved** | One purchase order was saved as a **Draft**. It still needs your separate purchasing review. |
| **Open record** | Opens the customer, order, refund or purchase order in the portal. It appears only when your role can open that page. |

The result card shows the time the action ran on the server. If your connection drops right after you approve, the card offers **Verify action result** instead of a second approval, so you can confirm what happened without running it again.

## Saving a purchase order draft

Ask the assistant to prepare a purchase order draft for one store and one supplier. Give the product variants, whole-number quantities and each unit cost. You can include up to 20 different variants. If facts are missing, the assistant asks you to supply them. It does not use the selling price as a supplier quote or treat a missing cost as zero.

1. Check the store, supplier and every line on the confirmation card. Review the quantity, unit cost, currency, tax, shipping and total. Pay particular attention to any line with zero cost and any tax or shipping shown as zero.
2. Check when the facts were read and when the review expires. Ask for a fresh preview if you change the supplier, items, quantities or costs.
3. Choose **Approve** to save one **Draft** purchase order. The **Action result** shows its number, total and saved status. **Open record** appears when your role can view purchase orders.

Saving the draft does not send it to the supplier, approve the purchase order, receive stock or make a payment. Review and complete those steps separately in [Purchase orders](/docs/suppliers/purchase-orders).

Restock estimates may exclude incoming purchase quantities, supplier quotes and delivery lead times. The preview identifies unavailable facts. Check them before deciding what to order. If the connection drops after approval, use **Verify action result** to recover the saved result. Approving the same card again returns the same draft.

## Reviewed help articles

Ask how to use a supported feature and the assistant can look up reviewed public guides in your language. Its answer links to the source guide. If there is no current reviewed guidance for your question, it says so; use **Get human help** to contact support. These answers do not include private support cases or staff notes.

## Structured input forms

When the assistant needs missing details before it can continue (for example refund amount, refund method, or an optional reason), it shows a compact **input card** in the thread instead of asking the same questions only in prose.

- Answer with suggested choices or use **Other** for a custom text or number when offered. Numbers can be typed with Arabic-Indic digits (for example ١٢٫٥٠); they are read the same as 12.50.
- Optional fields are marked and may be left blank.
- Tap **Continue** to submit. The assistant validates your answers and resumes the task.
- You can still type a normal message in the composer. That message counts as your answer and disables the earlier card.
- Submitting the form only supplies parameters. Mutations such as refunds still require a separate **Approve** step on the confirmation card.

## Daily usage limit

Your plan includes a daily **token** budget for assistant messages. A progress strip above the message box shows how much remains. Usage counts what replies actually used, plus room held for replies that are still running. If the AI service does not report how much a reply used, the assistant counts the full amount it held for that reply, so usage is never understated.

When the limit is reached:

- You cannot send new messages or answer input cards until the limit resets; the card shows why
- **New conversation** and suggested prompts are disabled
- Your conversations, results and approval receipts stay available, and you can still archive threads

If the limit is reached in the middle of a long reply, the assistant keeps the part it already finished and marks the answer as incomplete.

The daily budget resets at midnight **UTC**. The strip shows the exact reset time in your local time.

## Ask a person for help

If an answer fails or you are unsure what happened after an action, open the conversation and choose **Get human help** above the message box. This works even after you reach the assistant's daily usage limit.

1. Write a subject and describe what happened. Include the current store only if it helps support investigate.
2. Optionally select the conversation excerpts you want to share. No message is selected for you. You can select up to ten visible messages.
3. Choose **Review what will be shared**. Check the **Public support message** and the separate **Support staff note**. The note includes a short page and connection diagnostic. Messages that cannot be shared are identified without adding guessed text.
4. Choose **Send to support** only when both sections look right. If the conversation changes before you send, review the updated content again.

The request first receives a case reference. **Received** means Cashvio accepted it, while **Sending to support** means delivery is still in progress. **With support** means the delivery completed. Use **Open case status** to check it again. If sending fails or needs a staff check, the status says so. If the response to **Send to support** is lost, retry from the same review screen so the request keeps its reference.

Asking for help does not approve a pending assistant action or run it again. You can close the form without sending anything.

## Managing conversations

- **Search**: when you have many threads, use the search box in the sidebar. It searches the titles of all your conversations, not just the ones loaded on screen.
- If the conversation list cannot load, the sidebar says so and offers **Retry**; it never shows an empty history by mistake.
- In a long conversation, use **Load older messages** at the top. If that page cannot load, the messages already shown stay available and an inline **Retry** lets you try again.
- **Archive**: hide a conversation from the sidebar. Archived conversations can later be removed under the retention settings. Archiving is not permanent storage or immediate erasure; unsettled actions and their evidence can be kept longer.
- **Copy**: use the copy button under any assistant reply to grab its text.

## Tips for better answers

- Name the store or product when you manage more than one location or have similar item names.
- For analytics, mention the date range you care about (today, this week, last 30 days).
- If the assistant asks you to pick between similar orders or products, reply with the number or name it shows.
- After a list, you can refer to items by position ("the second one"). The assistant remembers recent lists in the conversation; if it is not sure which list you mean, it asks. To see those lists, open the conversation's menu and choose **What the assistant remembers**; **Clear memory** makes it forget them while keeping your messages, results and AI usage.
- Ask for **more** after a list to see the next page instead of the same results again.
- Name another store and the assistant looks it up among the stores you can use. If the store is not available to you, it says so and asks which store you mean instead of using a different one.
