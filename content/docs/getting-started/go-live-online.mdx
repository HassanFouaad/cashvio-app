---
title: Go Live Online
description: "Turn on your Cashvio free online store subdomain and take your first online order."
---

When your in-store setup is working, you can publish a free online storefront on a Cashvio subdomain. Shoppers browse, check out, and track orders without a separate website builder.

## Open Online Store

In the sidebar, go to **Online Store**. You land on the storefront settings for the active store. If you run multiple locations, pick the store whose subdomain you want to publish.

## Set your subdomain

Choose a short, memorable subdomain (for example, `mystore.cash-vio.com`). Cashvio supports subdomain storefronts only; custom domains are not available yet.

Save the subdomain before you continue. It becomes the link you share with customers.

## Appearance basics

Under storefront appearance, add at least one hero image and confirm your store name and contact details show correctly. Optional but helpful: link [WhatsApp](/docs/online-store/social-whatsapp-button) so shoppers can message you from the storefront.

See [Storefront Appearance](/docs/online-store/storefront-appearance) for banners, colors, and featured products.

## Activate the storefront

Turn the online store **on** when you are ready for real orders. Active products from your catalogue appear automatically. Out-of-stock rules and delivery zones follow the store settings you configured under **Settings > Stores**.

<Callout title="Before you go live">
Confirm [portal payment methods](/docs/stores/portal-payment-methods) and [fulfillment methods](/docs/stores/fulfillment-methods) match how you want online orders paid and delivered.
</Callout>

## Place a test order

Open your storefront URL on your phone (or share it with a teammate). Add a product to the cart, complete [checkout](/docs/online-store/checkout-flow), and place a test order. This confirms prices, delivery or pickup, and payment settings end to end.

## Track the order

Find the test order under **Orders** in the portal. Update its status and try [order tracking](/docs/online-store/order-tracking) from the customer side so you know what shoppers see after they buy.
