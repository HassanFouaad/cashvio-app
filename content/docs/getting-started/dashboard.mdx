## Your daily workspace

**Home** brings your everyday work together. The welcome panel shows today's date, your available sales actions, and **Today's sales** when analytics is enabled. Today's figures stay focused on today even when you change the period further down the page.

Choose **New Sale** to open the POS or **View Sales** to review orders. **Invite a shop owner** opens the sharing options for your signup link.

## Needs attention

Each tile shows a count and opens the relevant list, such as pending orders, overdue payments, returns, low stock, unpaid orders, or discounts. You only see items available to your role and enabled features. The section is hidden when there are no items to show.

If the counts cannot load, a message appears instead. Use **Retry** when available.

## Quick Actions

- **Add Product** opens the product creation page.
- **Manage Inventory** opens your stock list.
- **Add Customer** opens the customer form.

Each shortcut includes a short explanation. Actions depend on your permissions and enabled features.

## Sales Overview

Choose **Today**, **This Week**, or **This Month** beside **Sales Overview** to change its four metrics: revenue, orders, average order value, and gross profit. The update time tells you when these figures last loaded. Comparisons appear when baseline data is available.

A period without orders shows a clear message. A failed request shows recovery options rather than implying there were no sales. If a refresh fails temporarily, earlier figures remain visible with a warning that they may be out of date. Use **Retry** to update them without changing the selected period.

Choose **View reports** for [sales trends and detailed reports](/docs/reports/sales-overview-report). Charts are in Reports.

## Store and app shortcuts

At the bottom, eligible accounts may see app installation and **Your store is live** banners. Each banner can be dismissed separately. The store banner lets you open your storefront or copy its link.

## Moving around

Use the sidebar on desktop or the bottom navigation on mobile. **More** opens additional destinations on mobile. Press **Ctrl+K** or **Cmd+K** to search for a page or action. Keyboard users can use the skip link to reach the main content and Tab to move through actions.

Home follows your selected language and theme. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. All sections adapt to your permissions and enabled features.
