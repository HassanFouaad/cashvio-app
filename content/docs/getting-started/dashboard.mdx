## What is the Dashboard?

The **Dashboard** is the first screen you see after logging in. It greets you, shows how your business is doing for the selected period, highlights anything that needs your attention, and gives you shortcuts to the most common actions.

At the top right of the Dashboard you can switch the time period for all numbers and charts: **Today**, **This Week**, or **This Month**.

## Banners

Directly under the greeting, the Dashboard may show **one dismissible banner** with your most important next step. Banners appear in this order:

1. **Your store is live**: shows your online store link right after signup so you can view it, copy it, and replace the sample products with your own.
2. **Make your first sale**: a one-time nudge that opens the sale (POS) screen directly. It disappears as soon as you record your first order.
3. **Install the app**: an invitation to install Cashvio on your device for quick access and notifications.

Once you dismiss a banner or complete its action, the next one takes its place.

## Quick Actions

A row of quick-action tiles gives you one-tap access to the most common tasks:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **New Sale** | Opens the full-screen sale (POS) screen |
| **View Orders** | Opens the orders list |
| **View Products** | Opens the product catalogue |
| **Manage Inventory** | Opens the inventory list |
| **Add Customer** | Opens the new-customer form |
| **Invite a shop owner** | Opens a WhatsApp-first share sheet with your personal signup link (see [Invite Shop Owners](/docs/getting-started/invite-shop-owners)) |

<Callout title="Important">
The Dashboard adapts to your **permissions and plan features**. You only see quick actions and sections for the things you are allowed to access.
</Callout>

## Needs Attention

The **Needs Attention** list shows urgent items with live counts, each linking straight to the filtered list:

- **Pending orders**: orders waiting to be processed
- **Unpaid orders**: orders with outstanding payment
- **Overdue payments**: scheduled payments past their due date
- **Low stock**: products at or below their reorder point
- **Pending returns**: return requests awaiting review

If there is nothing urgent, this section stays hidden.

## Sales Overview

Four KPI cards summarize your performance for the selected period, each with a comparison against the previous period:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Payments received in the period |
| **Total Orders** | Number of orders created |
| **Average Order Value** | Revenue divided by the number of orders |
| **Gross Profit** | Revenue minus product costs |

Below the cards, two charts show your **store visits** and **top products**. Click **View All** to open the full [Reports](/docs/reports/analytics-reports) section.

## Sidebar Navigation

On the left side of the screen (right side in Arabic) is the **sidebar**, a flat list of the main sections plus a pinned **New Sale** button that is always available:

| Item | What it covers |
|------|----------------|
| **Dashboard** | The main overview |
| **Sales** | Orders, with in-page tabs for Returns, Refunds, and Overdue Payments |
| **Catalogue** | Products, with tabs for Categories, Options, and Coupons |
| **Inventory** | Stock, with tabs for Low Stock, Adjustments, Purchase Orders, and Suppliers |
| **Customers** | Customer list and profiles |
| **Online Store** | Your storefront settings |
| **Reports** | Analytics and reports |
| **Team** | Users, with a tab for Roles & Permissions |
| **Settings** | General settings, Stores, Channels, Plan & Features, and Account |

Related pages are grouped as **tabs at the top of each section** rather than sidebar submenus. For example, opening **Sales** shows tabs for Orders, Returns, Refunds, and Overdue Payments.

On **mobile devices**, the sidebar becomes a **bottom navigation bar** with the most important items. Tap **More** to reach everything else.

## Command Palette (Quick Search)

Press **Ctrl+K** (or **Cmd+K** on Mac), or click the **search icon** in the top bar, to open the **command palette**. Type to jump to any page or action (for example "refunds", "add product", or "delivery zones") without navigating menus.

## Language, Theme, and Support

In the **top bar** you will find:

- **Language switcher**: Switch between English and Arabic instantly.
- **Profile menu**: Toggle between **dark mode** and **light mode**, open your account settings, or log out.
- **Support**: Open the live support chat.
