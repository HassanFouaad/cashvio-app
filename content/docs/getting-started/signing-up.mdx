## How to Sign Up

To start using Cashvio, create an account in the Cashvio portal. Your free account includes a [free POS](/features/free-pos) (cashier) and a [free online store](/features/free-online-store), with no credit card required.

1. Click **Sign Up** or **Start Free** on the Cashvio website, or **Create an account** on the portal login page.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Business name**: The name of your business (your first store is created with it).
   - **Phone number**: Your contact number.
   - **Email**: A valid email you have access to. This will be your login.
   - **Password**: Choose a strong password (at least 8 characters).
3. Click **Create account**. A welcome page opens on the Cashvio website and takes you to your dashboard shortly; click **Go to Dashboard** to go right away. Your online store is live from that very moment. A welcome email arrives with your account details, your store link, and a **Confirm email address** button. See [Confirming Your Email](#confirming-your-email).

<Callout title="Already signed in?">
If this browser is already signed in to Cashvio, opening the sign-up form takes you to your dashboard instead. To create a separate business, sign out first, then open the sign-up form again. See [One Session per Browser](/docs/getting-started/login-and-sessions#one-session-per-browser).
</Callout>

## Signing Up with Google

If your email is a Gmail address or a Google Workspace address (a business email hosted by Google), you can sign up with your Google account instead of creating a password.

1. On the sign-up form, click **Continue with Google** above the fields.
2. Choose your Google account in the Google window.
3. The **Create your account** form shows your Google email. Enter your **Business name** and **Phone number**.
4. Click **Create account**. The welcome page opens and takes you to your dashboard, the same as signing up with email.

**Continue with Google** on the portal login page opens the same form when your Google account has no Cashvio account.

To choose another account or use email and password, click **Change account**.

<Callout title="Joining your team's business?">
Click **Sign in** below the form and use the username your business owner gave you. You can then connect Google from [Account Settings](/docs/settings/account-settings#google).
</Callout>

<Callout title="No password needed">
You don't create a password when you sign up with Google. To sign in, click **Continue with Google** on the login page. You can set a password at any time with **Forgot password?** on the login page, or **Set a password** in [Account Settings](/docs/settings/account-settings#set-a-password). After that, you can sign in either way.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Other email addresses">
Google can only confirm Gmail and Google Workspace addresses. If your Google account uses any other email, the sign-up form switches to the email and password fields with that email filled in. Choose a password and finish signing up, then connect Google later from [Account Settings](/docs/settings/account-settings#google).
</Callout>

If a Cashvio account already uses your Google account or your Gmail address, **Continue with Google** signs you in to that account instead of creating a new business. See [Signing In with Google](/docs/getting-started/login-and-sessions#signing-in-with-google).

## Confirming Your Email

Cashvio emails you only after you confirm that your email address is yours. You can use all of Cashvio before you confirm it, but until then Cashvio sends you no emails, such as security alerts and account notices. Password reset links and confirmation links are the only exception.

To confirm your email:

1. Open the welcome email and click **Confirm email address**.
2. A page opens and shows **Email confirmed**. Click **Continue to Cashvio**, or **Sign in** if this browser isn't signed in.

Until you confirm your email, a **Confirm your email address** window opens every time you load the portal and after every sign-in:

- **Resend link** sends you a new confirmation link. You can ask for another one after a minute, up to 5 an hour; every link you received keeps working.
- **Wrong address? Change your email** opens [Account Settings](/docs/settings/account-settings#changing-your-email), where you can fix a mistyped address.
- **Later** hides the window until you reload the page or sign in again.

Your email is also confirmed when you:

- Sign up with Google, or sign in with Google using the same Gmail or Google Workspace address.
- Reset your password from the emailed link.
- Change your email and confirm the new address from its verification email.
- Accept a team invitation from its email. See [Adding a Team Member](/docs/team/users#adding-a-team-member).

<Callout title="Link expired?">
A confirmation link works for 7 days. If it no longer works, sign in and click **Resend link** in the confirmation window, or next to your email in **Settings > Account**.
</Callout>

## Logging In

1. Go to the Cashvio portal login page.
2. Enter your **email or username** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

If you signed up with Google or connected Google to your account, click **Continue with Google** instead and choose your Google account.

You will be taken to your **Dashboard**, the main screen of Cashvio.

For session expiry, sign out, and recovery details, see [Login and Sessions](/docs/getting-started/login-and-sessions).

<Callout title="Team members">
If you were added by your business owner, log in with the **username and temporary password** they shared with you.
</Callout>

## Forgot Your Password?

If you forget your password:

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your **email or username** and submit. A password reset link is sent to your email.
3. Open the email and click the reset link.
4. Enter your **new password** (at least 8 characters) and confirm it.
5. Click **Reset password**.

You can now log in with your new password. If you signed up with Google, these same steps set your first password.

## Changing Your Language

Cashvio supports **English** and **Arabic**. To switch your language:

1. Look at the top of the screen for the **language selector**.
2. Click it and choose your preferred language (**English** or **العربية**).
3. The entire interface will switch to the selected language immediately.

<Callout title="Note">
The language setting applies to your session. Each user can choose their own preferred language independently.
</Callout>
