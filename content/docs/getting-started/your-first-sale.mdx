This page is a quick bridge from setup to your first completed order. If you already finished [Quick Setup](/docs/getting-started/onboarding), you are one screen away from ringing up a sale.

## Before you start

You need at least one active product in your catalogue. If you have not added one yet, follow [Create Product](/docs/catalogue/create-product) first.

## Open New Sale

Start the register from any of these entry points:

- Click **New Sale** in the sidebar (always pinned).
- Use the **New Sale** quick action on the [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard).
- On mobile, tap the round **New Sale** button in the center of the bottom bar.

The sale screen opens full screen. If you manage multiple stores, choose the store first so prices and payment methods match that location.

## Add a product and charge

1. Browse categories or search, then **tap a product** to add it to the cart.
2. Adjust quantity in the cart if needed.
3. Tap **Charge** (or the payment button) and pick a payment method.
4. Confirm the sale. Cashvio creates the order and shows a receipt.

<Callout title="Tip">
Run a test sale with a small amount while you are learning. You can void or refund later if your role allows it.
</Callout>

## After the sale

Open the order from **Orders** to print or share the receipt, update fulfillment, or attach a customer retroactively.
