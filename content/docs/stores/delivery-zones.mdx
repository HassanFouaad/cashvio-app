## What are Delivery Zones?

Delivery zones define **where your store delivers** and **how much delivery costs** per area. When a delivery order is placed (in the portal or on the storefront), the customer's country and city are matched against your zones to determine the delivery fee.

## Default Delivery Fee

At the top of the Delivery Zones tab you can set the store's **default delivery fee** — used when a delivery address doesn't match any zone. You can edit it inline or clear it entirely.

## How to Set Up Delivery Zones

1. Go to **Settings > Stores**, open your store, and select the **Delivery Zones** tab.
2. Click **Add Delivery Zone** and fill in:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Country** | The country this zone covers (pre-selected from your store's country) |
| **City** | The city within that country |
| **Fee Type** | **Fixed** amount or **Percentage** of the order |
| **Fee Value** | The delivery fee amount or percentage |
| **Active** | Whether the zone is currently enabled |

3. Click **Save**. Each zone covers **one country + city pair** — add a zone per city you deliver to.

## Managing Delivery Zones

- **Edit a zone** — update its fee or active status.
- **Delete a zone** — remove areas you no longer deliver to.
- **Multiple zones** — add as many zones as you need to cover your delivery area.

## How Delivery Zones Affect the Portal

When [creating a delivery order](/docs/orders/creating-orders):

1. The delivery address is matched to a zone.
2. The corresponding **delivery fee** is added to the order automatically.
3. You can **manually override** the fee on any individual order.

## How Delivery Zones Affect the Storefront

When a customer places a delivery order on your [online store](/docs/online-store/checkout-flow):

1. The **Country** and **City** dropdowns are driven by your configured zones.
2. The delivery fee applies automatically based on the selected zone.

<Callout title="Important">
Setting up delivery zones is recommended if you offer delivery through the storefront. It ensures customers only select areas you actually deliver to, with the correct fee applied automatically.
</Callout>
