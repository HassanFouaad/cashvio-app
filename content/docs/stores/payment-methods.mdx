## What are Payment Methods?

Payment methods define how customers can pay for their orders. Each store has two sets of payment methods:

- **Portal Payment Methods**: Used when you or your team create orders in the business portal (like a POS system).
- **Storefront Payment Methods**: Used when customers place orders through your online store.

## Available Payment Methods

Cashvio uses a unified set of payment methods:

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Physical cash payment. The system calculates change automatically when creating portal orders. For storefront orders, the customer pays on delivery or pickup. |
| **Online** | Digital payment (credit/debit card, InstaPay, mobile wallet, bank transfer, etc.). |
| **Receipt** | The payer uploads a photo of the payment receipt as proof of transfer. |
| **Store Credit** | Using the customer's store credit balance. Only available in the portal when a customer is selected and has credit. |

## Portal-Only Payment Features

These apply when your team creates or collects payment in the business portal:

- **Store credit**: Appears automatically when the selected customer has a balance. See [Store Credit](/docs/customers/store-credit) and [Portal Payment Methods](/docs/stores/portal-payment-methods).
- **Split payments**: Pay part of an order with store credit and the rest with cash or another method. Details in [Portal Payment Methods](/docs/stores/portal-payment-methods).
- **Scheduled payments**: Leave a balance due on a future date. See [Portal Payment Methods](/docs/stores/portal-payment-methods) and [Overdue Payments](/docs/orders/overdue-payments).
