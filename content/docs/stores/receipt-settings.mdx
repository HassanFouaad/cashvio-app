## What are Receipt Settings?

Receipt settings control the **printed and digital receipt** that accompanies each order: what appears on it, which printer it uses, and whether it prints automatically after each sale.

## How to Configure Receipt Settings

1. Go to **Settings > Stores**, open your store, and select the **Receipt** tab. (With one store, the Settings hub's "Receipt Settings" link takes you straight there.)
2. Adjust the settings and click **Save**.

## What You Can Customize

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Printer** | Pair a thermal printer, run a **Test print**, or **Open cash drawer** before a live sale |
| **Show Header** | Whether to show a custom header section at the top |
| **Header Text (English / Arabic)** | Custom header text in both languages, shown when the header is on |
| **Footer Text (English / Arabic)** | Custom footer text in both languages (e.g., "Thank you for your visit!"); required |
| **Show Logo** | Whether to include your business logo |
| **Show Store Address** | Whether to print the store's address |
| **Show Store Phone** | Whether to print the store's phone number |
| **VAT Registration Number** | Your tax registration number, printed under the store details on every receipt |
| **Commercial Registration Number** | Your commercial registration number, printed next to the VAT number |
| **Auto Print After Order** | Automatically print the receipt when a sale is completed on the [New Sale screen](/docs/orders/creating-orders) |

<Callout title="Note">
The store name, address, and phone values come from your [store settings](/docs/stores/creating-store); here you only choose whether to show them. The receipt is rendered in the language you are using (English or Arabic), with your bilingual header/footer texts. For the full VAT setup, including the default tax rate and the tax report, see [VAT Setup and Tax Reports](/docs/stores/taxes-and-vat).
</Callout>

## Pair and Qualify Your Printer

Set up the printer on the **Receipt** tab so you are not choosing a device mid-sale:

1. Open the **Printer** card at the top of Receipt settings. If no printer is paired yet, it says "No printer paired for this store on this device".
2. Choose the **Paper width** (**58 mm** or **80 mm**) to match your thermal roll, and the **Connection type** (**Serial / COM** or **USB**) your printer uses. Your connection choice is used the next time you pair; select USB if your printer does not appear in the serial picker.
3. Tap **Pair printer** and select your printer in the browser device prompt. Cancelling the prompt keeps your existing setup unchanged.
4. Tap **Test print** and confirm the message: this test sends a non-sale receipt without opening the drawer.
5. Inspect the paper: both languages, alignment, and nothing clipped at the edges. Then tap **I checked the test output** to record that this printer is qualified for your counter.
6. Optionally tap **Open cash drawer** to confirm the kick cable.

<Callout title="Important">
Remembered on this browser does not mean connected or ready. Use **Check connection**, then print a test before serving customers. If two identical printers are connected, the app asks you to choose again instead of guessing — disconnect the extras and pair the intended printer.
</Callout>

If you change the paper width, the change is saved on this browser immediately — print a new test to check alignment.

If printing fails, check the message on screen:

| Message | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| No thermal printer is connected. Turn it on and connect it, or choose a different printer. | Turn the printer on, connect it, then print again |
| The remembered printer is not connected. Reconnect it or pair again. | Plug in the same printer or pair again |
| Couldn't reach the thermal printer. Check that it's powered on and connected, then try again. | Reconnect USB or power-cycle the printer |
| More than one matching printer is available. Choose the intended device again. | Unplug extras, pair again, keep one connected |
| This printer is finishing another job. Wait a moment, then try again. | Wait for the current job to finish |
| The printer may have received part of the job. Check the paper, then use Reprint if needed. | Check the paper first — do not reprint blindly |
| This browser cannot talk to a thermal printer. Use Chrome or Edge on a device with USB or a paired Bluetooth COM port. | Switch to a supported browser |
| This printer profile is incomplete. Pair the printer again and confirm the paper width. | Pair again and confirm the width |

## Printing Receipts

You can print a receipt from the sale success screen (**Print receipt**) or from any order's details:

1. Open the **Order Details** page and click **Print / Share** in the header.
2. Choose **Print**. The first time, you pick the print method:
   - **Thermal printer**: prints directly to a connected receipt printer
   - **Browser print**: opens the standard print dialog
3. Your choice is remembered per store (you can reset it with **Remove** next to the paired printer).

Every attempt — including failed ones — appears in the screen-corner **Print jobs** list; see [Print and Share Receipts](/docs/orders/print-share-receipts).

The same dialog also offers **PDF**, a **shareable receipt link**, and a **QR code** for the customer.

## Payments on the Receipt

Completed payments appear in the settlement section with their method (Cash, Online, and so on). **Pending** payments are listed separately and labeled **Pending** so staff can see money still owed. Failed or cancelled payment attempts are not printed.

If receipt settings have not been saved yet, printing uses the default template: store name, address and phone, order and payment details — no custom logo, header, footer or tax registration fields. Save your settings to customize this.

<Callout title="Tip">
Enable **Auto Print After Order** for counter sales so the receipt prints itself the moment each sale completes. Pair the printer and confirm a test output first so auto-print does not pause for a device chooser.
</Callout>
