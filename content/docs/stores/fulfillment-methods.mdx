## What are Fulfillment Methods?

Fulfillment methods define **how orders are delivered to customers**. When creating an order in the business portal or when a customer checks out on your online store, a fulfillment method is selected. Each store can enable different methods.

## Available Fulfillment Methods

| Method | Description | Available On |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **In-Store** | The customer purchases and receives the product at the store. | Portal only |
| **Pickup** | The customer places the order and picks it up at the store later. | Portal + Storefront |
| **Delivery** | The order is delivered to the customer's address. | Portal + Storefront |
| **Dine-In** | The customer orders and eats at the store (for restaurants/cafes). | Portal only |

## How to Configure Fulfillment Methods

Fulfillment methods are configured **separately for each channel**, just like payment methods:

- **Portal**: the methods your staff can choose when creating orders from the business portal or POS.
- **Storefront (Online)**: the methods your customers see at online checkout.

1. Go to **Settings > Stores**, open your store, and select the **Fulfillment** tab.
2. Switch between the **Storefront (Online)** and **Portal** tabs to manage each channel.
3. Enable or disable each method using its toggle (each change asks for confirmation).

Changes to one channel never affect the other: disabling Delivery for the storefront doesn't stop your staff from creating delivery orders in the portal.

## Delivery Fulfillment

When **Delivery** is enabled, creating an order with delivery requires:

- A **delivery address** (country, city, additional details)
- The system can apply **delivery fees** based on the delivery zone

See [Delivery Zones](/docs/stores/delivery-zones) for setting up delivery areas and fees.

## Storefront Fulfillment

On your online storefront, customers can only be offered **Delivery** and **Pickup**. Make sure at least one of these is enabled on the **Storefront (Online)** tab if you want customers to place orders through the storefront.

<Callout title="Important">
**In-Store** can never be offered on the online storefront; it is a staff-only method for walk-in sales at the counter. The system rejects any attempt to enable it for online checkout, and online orders can never use it.
</Callout>

## Order Status Tracking

Pickup, Delivery, and Dine-In orders follow a status flow that you update from the **Order Details** page:

**Pickup / Dine-In:**
1. **Pending**: Order created, waiting to be prepared
2. **Preparing**: Order is being prepared
3. **Ready**: Order is ready for the customer
4. **Completed**: Customer received the order

**Delivery:**
1. **Pending**: Order created
2. **Preparing**: Order is being prepared
3. **Ready**: Ready for dispatch
4. **Delivering**: Out for delivery
5. **Completed**: Delivered to customer

You can move directly to a later status (e.g., from Pending straight to Completed), and at any stage the fulfillment can be **Cancelled**.

<Callout title="Note">
**In-Store** orders have no fulfillment tracking; the customer receives the items at the counter, so there is nothing to track.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Tip">
Stay on top of fulfillment status updates. Promptly moving orders through the status flow helps keep your operations organized, especially when managing many orders from both the portal and the storefront simultaneously.
</Callout>
