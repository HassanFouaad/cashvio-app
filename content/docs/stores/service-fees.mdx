## What are Service Fees?

Service fees are additional charges added to orders — for example, a restaurant's 10% table-service charge. Fees appear as a separate line item in the order summary, keeping pricing transparent for the customer.

## How to Configure Service Fees

1. Go to **Settings > Stores**, open your store, and select the **Service Fees** tab.
2. Click **Add Fee** and configure it:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Fee Type** | **Fixed** (a set amount) or **Percentage** (a share of the subtotal, up to 100%) |
| **Fee Value** | The amount or the percentage |

3. Click **Save**.

A store can have **multiple service fees** — each appears in the fees table where you can **edit** or **delete** it.

## How Service Fees Appear on Orders

When an order is created for a store with service fees:

- The fee is added to the order automatically
- It appears as **Service Fee** in the order summary
- Percentage fees are calculated from the subtotal

**Example:**

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Subtotal | 200 EGP |
| Service Fee (10%) | 20 EGP |
| Tax (14%) | 30.80 EGP |
| **Total** | **250.80 EGP** |

See [Pricing & Tax Calculations](/docs/calculations/pricing-tax-calculations) for a full breakdown of how totals are computed.

## Overriding Service Fees on an Order

When [making a sale](/docs/orders/creating-orders), you can **manually adjust** the fee for that order:

1. In the cart summary, open the **Fees** option.
2. Enter the new amount.
3. The order total updates automatically.

<Callout title="Note">
Overriding the fee only affects the current order. The default fee configuration stays unchanged for future orders.
</Callout>
