title: Storefront Payment Methods
description: "Configure Cash, Receipt, and Online payments for storefront checkout and set a minimum order value."

Storefront payment methods control what options customers see during online checkout. By default, **Cash** and **Receipt** are enabled for all stores. **Online Payment** becomes available once you connect a payment gateway.

Configure them from **Settings > Stores**, open your store, and select the **Payments** tab, then the **Storefront** section.

## How Each Method Works on the Storefront

| Method | Customer Experience |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Cash** | Customer selects "Cash" at checkout. A pending payment record is created. You mark it as paid from the business portal when the customer pays on delivery or pickup. |
| **Receipt** | Customer selects "Upload Receipt" and uploads a photo of their payment transfer. A pending payment is created with the receipt attached. You review and accept the receipt from the business portal. |
| **Online Payment** | Customer selects "Pay Online" and is redirected to a secure payment page to pay by card or wallet. The order is marked paid automatically the moment the payment is confirmed. No manual review needed. |

<Callout title="Online payments need a gateway first">
You can only enable **Online Payment** after connecting a payment gateway (such as Paymob) to the store. See [Online Payments with Paymob](/docs/integrations/paymob-online-payments) for the full setup guide.
</Callout>

See [Online Checkout Flow](/docs/online-store/checkout-flow) for the full customer journey and [Order Payments](/docs/orders/order-payments) for accepting receipts and marking cash paid from the portal.

## Minimum Order Value

The Payments tab also holds the store's **Minimum Order Value** setting. When set, storefront customers must reach this amount before they can place an order. Set it to the value you need and save, or leave it at 0 for no minimum.

### What customers see

When a minimum is set, the storefront **cart page** actively nudges customers toward it:

- A message: **"Add X more to place your order"** with the exact remaining amount
- A **progress bar** filling up as they add items
- The checkout button stays **disabled** until the minimum is reached

This turns the minimum from a checkout-time surprise into a gentle upsell: customers add one more item instead of abandoning the order.
