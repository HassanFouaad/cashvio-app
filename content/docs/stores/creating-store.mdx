## What is a Store?

A **store** in Cashvio represents a business location where you sell products. This can be a physical shop, a warehouse, an online store, or any point of sale. Each store has its own:

- **Currency** — the currency used for pricing and transactions
- **Payment methods** — which payment methods are accepted
- **Fulfillment methods** — how orders are delivered or picked up
- **Inventory** — stock levels tracked separately per store

When you sign up for Cashvio, your first store is **automatically created** using your business details. If your plan supports multiple stores, you can add more later.

## Opening Your Store

1. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar — your stores appear as cards at the top (or open **Settings > Stores** for the full list).
2. Click a store to open its page. Everything about the store is organized as **tabs**:

| Tab | What it configures |
|-----|-------------------|
| **Overview** | Read-only summary: name, code, currency, contact info, and address |
| **Settings** | Edit the store's basic information |
| **[Payments](/docs/stores/payment-methods)** | Payment methods for the portal and the storefront |
| **[Fulfillment](/docs/stores/fulfillment-methods)** | In-Store, Pickup, Delivery, Dine-In |
| **[Delivery Zones](/docs/stores/delivery-zones)** | Delivery areas and fees |
| **[Service Fees](/docs/stores/service-fees)** | Additional fees added to orders |
| **[Receipt](/docs/stores/receipt-settings)** | Receipt content and auto-print |
| **Channels** | Which sales channels are active for this store |
| **[POS](/docs/stores/pos-devices)** | POS devices and pairing |
| **[Online Store](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview)** | Your storefront settings |

<Callout title="Shortcut">
The Settings hub also has quick-access links for each of these areas. If you have one store they open it directly; with multiple stores you pick the store first.
</Callout>

## Adding a Store

1. Go to **Settings > Stores** and click **Add Store**.
2. Fill in the details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Store Name** | A name to identify this store (e.g., "Main Branch") — required |
| **Currency** | The currency for this store's transactions — required |
| **Active** | Whether the store starts active |
| **Contact Email / Phone** | The store's contact details |
| **Country / City / Address / Postal Code** | The store's location |

3. Click **Save**. The number of stores you can create depends on your plan.

## Editing Store Settings

Open the store's **Settings** tab to update the name, currency, contact details, and address. The store **code** is generated automatically and cannot be changed.

You can also **Activate** or **Deactivate** the store from this tab — deactivated stores stop accepting new activity without losing any data.

## Multi-Store Operations

If your plan supports multiple stores:

- **Each store tracks inventory independently.** A product can have different stock levels in each store.
- **Orders belong to a specific store.** When making a sale, you pick the store first.
- **Prices can differ per store.** Set store-specific prices per product variant.
- **Reports break down by store.** See sales and profit per store inside the reports.
- **Users can be limited to specific stores** via store-scoped roles.

<Callout title="Tip">
You can transfer inventory between stores. See [Adjustments & Transfers](/docs/inventory/adjustments-transfers) for details.
</Callout>
