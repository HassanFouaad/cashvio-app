## What is the catalog integration?

The Facebook and Instagram Catalog integration publishes your online store products as a live feed link that Meta reads automatically. Once connected, your products, prices, photos, and stock levels appear in **Meta Commerce Manager**, ready for catalog ads, Advantage+ campaigns, and product tagging on Facebook and Instagram posts. There is nothing to re-upload when products change: Meta refetches the link on a schedule and stays in sync on its own.

<Callout title="Store level integration">
The catalog is connected per store. Each store with an online storefront gets its own feed link with its own products, prices, and stock.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Your store needs an active online storefront, since every catalog item links back to its product page.
- You need a free Meta Business account with access to [Commerce Manager](https://business.facebook.com/commerce/).
- Products need at least one photo. Meta rejects items without images, so products with no photos are left out of the feed.

## What the feed contains

| Catalog field | Comes from |
|---------------|------------|
| **Title and description** | The product name and description |
| **Price** | The store price if one is set, otherwise the base selling price, in your store currency |
| **Availability** | Live stock at this store: in stock or sold out |
| **Link** | The product page on your online store |
| **Images** | The product photos, main photo first |
| **Brand** | The brand name you set on the integration, or the store name |
| **Variants** | Each variant is its own catalog item, grouped under the product, with size and color when your options use those names |

## Connect the catalog

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations** in the business portal.
2. On the **Facebook & Instagram Catalog** card, click **Connect**.
3. Choose the **Store** to connect.
4. Optionally set a **Brand name**. It shows on every catalog item; leave it empty to use the store name.
5. Save. No keys or passwords are needed: saving creates your private **feed link** and opens the settings page where you can copy it.

## Add the feed link in Commerce Manager

1. In [Commerce Manager](https://business.facebook.com/commerce/), create a catalog of type **E-commerce**, or open an existing one.
2. Go to **Catalog > Data sources** and click **Add items**.
3. Choose **Data feed**, then **Scheduled feed**.
4. Paste your feed link, pick an update schedule (daily works well; hourly is the fastest Meta allows), and save.

Meta downloads your products right away and then refetches the link on the schedule you picked. You can trigger an extra fetch anytime from the data source settings with **Request update now**.

<Callout title="Keep the link private">
The feed link contains a long random token instead of a password. Anyone with the link can read your public product list, so only paste it into Commerce Manager. Deleting the integration invalidates the link immediately.
</Callout>

## Which products are included

A product appears in the feed when all of these are true:

- The product is **Active** and **Available on online store**.
- It has at least one photo.
- If your storefront hides out-of-stock products, the product is in stock. Otherwise out-of-stock products are included and Meta shows them as sold out.

The feed reflects changes within about 15 minutes; Meta then picks them up on its next scheduled fetch.

## Works with the Facebook Pixel

If you also connected the [Facebook Pixel](/docs/integrations/facebook-pixel), catalog ads and retargeting work out of the box: the pixel reports the same product identity the feed publishes, so Meta can show visitors the exact products they viewed or added to cart.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Meta says the feed URL cannot be reached** | Check the integration is **Active** in **Settings > Integrations** and that your store and its online storefront are active. |
| **Some products are missing from the catalog** | Confirm the product is Active, available on the online store, and has at least one photo. If your storefront hides out-of-stock items, restock the product. |
| **Prices or stock look outdated in Meta** | Meta refetches on your chosen schedule. Use **Request update now** in Commerce Manager to fetch immediately. |
| **Items rejected for missing images** | Add at least one photo to the product; it joins the feed automatically. |
