## September 2026

### Portal, POS & online store

- **Catalogue discounts**: mark variants, products, categories, or a whole store down on a schedule. Sale prices apply automatically at POS and on your storefront, stack with coupon codes, and feed `sale_price` to Meta. Total discount rates in sales reports include these markdowns, while staff discount reports exclude automatic markdowns so cashier metrics stay accurate. [Learn more](/docs/marketing/catalogue-discounts)

## August 2026

### Portal

- **AI Assistant**: chat in the merchant portal about orders, inventory, customers, and dashboard metrics. Mutating actions show an approval card before they run. A daily token meter shows remaining usage. [Learn more](/docs/getting-started/ai-assistant)

## July 2026

### Online store

- **Richer product link previews**: shared product links show price in the description and a 1200x630 image for WhatsApp and Facebook. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store)
- **Coupon deep links**: share `?coupon=CODE` on any storefront URL; checkout applies it automatically. [Learn more](/docs/marketing/apply-coupons)
- **Hide out of stock (end-to-end)**: the catalog and featured home products both honor **Hide Out of Stock**. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview)
- **Related products**: product pages show more items from the same category. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview)
- **WhatsApp chat button**: a floating WhatsApp button on every storefront page, with a dedicated WhatsApp number and an on/off toggle in Social Media Links. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/social-whatsapp-button)
- **Smarter order tracking**: the phone field prefills right after checkout, each status explains what happens next, and customers can WhatsApp or call you about their order. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/order-tracking)
- **Order tracking page**: customers can now follow their order status at `/track` on your storefront using the order number and phone. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/order-tracking)
- **Coupon codes at checkout**: shoppers can apply your coupons directly in the online checkout and see the discount before placing the order. [Learn more](/docs/marketing/apply-coupons)
- **Review stars on product cards**: average ratings now show on product listings, not just product pages. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/product-reviews)
- **Recently-viewed products**: a strip on your storefront helps shoppers pick up where they left off. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview)
- **Storefront themes**: pick one of 8 complete designs (Bistro, Tech, Boutique, and more) that restructure the header, product pages, order pages, mobile nav, and icons; pair any design with any color palette or build custom colors from your own brand color; and edit your logo, content, SEO, and social links beside the live preview in the fullscreen Theme Builder. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-themes)
- **Minimum-order progress**: carts show "add X more to place your order" instead of a surprise at checkout. [Learn more](/docs/stores/storefront-payment-methods)
- **Free delivery threshold**: set a minimum order amount that waives delivery; the cart shows "add X more for free delivery". [Learn more](/docs/stores/delivery-zones)
- **Search-engine improvements**: better language tags and structured data so your store ranks for both Arabic and English searches. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-seo-content)

### Portal & POS

- **Cafe/Restaurant Mode** (opt-in): enable **Cafe/Restaurant Mode** in **Settings > Features** (or Feature selection); it is off by default. Then you get Dine-In, optional table numbers on dine-in sales (set on New Sale or later on Order Details; storefront checkout does not ask for a table), service fees scoped by fulfillment method (for example 12% dine-in only), and a live **Kitchen** board that polls open tickets so staff can advance Preparing to Ready without leaving the line. [Kitchen board](/docs/orders/kitchen-board) · [Service fees](/docs/stores/service-fees) · [Table number on New Sale](/docs/orders/pos-cart-discounts)
- **Hold and resume tickets**: park a sale on New Sale, serve the next customer, then resume the held cart on the same register (up to 20 per device). [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-hold-tickets)
- **Item notes for the kitchen**: add a per-line note on New Sale (no sugar, extra shot) that prints on the receipt and shows on the Kitchen board. [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-cart-discounts)
- **Stocktake**: scan or type SKUs, set counted quantities, and save once to update stock with Manual Count adjustments. [Learn more](/docs/inventory/stocktake)
- **Barcode separate from SKU**: each variant has its own barcode field (EAN/UPC or generate an in-store code) so labels and scanners match the product packaging. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/create-product)
- **Camera barcode scanning**: tap Scan with camera on New Sale to add items with a phone or webcam, no USB scanner required. [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-barcode-scanning)
- **VAT setup and tax report**: set a default tax rate per store, print VAT and commercial registration numbers on receipts, and read the Tax summary in Reports. [Learn more](/docs/stores/taxes-and-vat)
- **Store link in your welcome email**: the signup email now includes your live store link, ready to share from minute one. Your storefront is also created instantly at signup, no waiting. [Learn more](/docs/getting-started/signing-up)
- **First-sale nudge**: after you check out your live store, the Dashboard shows a one-time shortcut straight into the sale screen until you record your first order. [Learn more](/docs/getting-started/dashboard)
- **Invite shop owners**: share your personal signup link from the Dashboard (WhatsApp-first) so other merchants can join Cashvio. [Learn more](/docs/getting-started/invite-shop-owners)
- **Scan-to-create at the register**: scanning a barcode that isn't in your catalogue opens **Quick Add Product** with the code prefilled as the SKU. Create it and it drops straight into the sale. Auto-generated SKUs now keep Arabic product names readable too. [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-barcode-scanning)
- **Today at this store**: from New Sale, open today's revenue, orders, and cash vs non-cash for the store you are on. [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-daily-summary)
- **Dead stock report**: see how much cash is sleeping on your shelves with 30 / 60 / 90 day windows. [Learn more](/docs/reports/dead-stock-analytics)
- **Fulfillment analytics**: see how sales split across in-store, pickup, delivery, and dine-in on the Reports > Fulfillment tab. [Learn more](/docs/reports/returns-fulfillment-reports)
- **Daily and weekly sales digests**: evening push with net sales and profit (screenshot-ready). [Learn more](/docs/settings/sales-digest-notifications)
- **Win-back reminders**: if your shop goes quiet for a week or more, Cashvio emails and pushes a short nudge back into New Sale. [Learn more](/docs/settings/win-back-notifications)
- **Subscription reminders and grace period**: get emailed before your plan ends, request an upgrade from Settings, and keep read-only access for about a week after expiry. [Learn more](/docs/settings/business-settings)
- **App icon badge**: installed apps show unread notification count on the home-screen icon. [Learn more](/docs/settings/account-settings#installing-the-app)
- **Native share for WhatsApp**: phones open the system share sheet before falling back to WhatsApp links. [Learn more](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing)
- **Product import from CSV / Excel**: bring your whole catalog in one upload with validation, progress tracking, and a ready-made template. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/importing-products)
- **Duplicate product**: copy an existing product with its options and variants as a starting point for a similar item. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/products)
- **Barcode label sheets**: print A4 sheets of scannable barcode labels for any product variant. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/barcode-labels)
- **WhatsApp receipts & order updates**: send digital receipts and status updates to customers in one tap. [Learn more](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing)
- **Store QR poster**: print a "scan to order" poster with your store QR code for the counter or window. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store)
- **Create purchase order from low stock**: turn low-stock alerts into a pre-filled purchase order with one click. [Learn more](/docs/inventory/low-stock-alerts)
- **Item-targeted coupons fixed**: coupons limited to specific products or categories now apply correctly at checkout. [Learn more](/docs/marketing/coupon-rules)
- **Arabic notifications**: in-app notifications now match your portal language.
