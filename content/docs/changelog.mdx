## July 2026

### Online store

- **WhatsApp chat button**: a floating WhatsApp button on every storefront page, with a dedicated WhatsApp number and an on/off toggle in Social Media Links. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview#social-media-links--whatsapp)
- **Smarter order tracking**: the phone field prefills right after checkout, each status explains what happens next, and customers can WhatsApp or call you about their order. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/order-tracking)
- **Order tracking page**: customers can now follow their order status at `/track` on your storefront using the order number and phone. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/order-tracking)
- **Coupon codes at checkout**: shoppers can apply your coupons directly in the online checkout and see the discount before placing the order.
- **Review stars on product cards**: average ratings now show on product listings, not just product pages.
- **Recently-viewed products**: a strip on your storefront helps shoppers pick up where they left off.
- **Your brand color everywhere**: set a primary color for your storefront so it stops looking like everyone else's.
- **Minimum-order progress**: carts show "add X more to place your order" instead of a surprise at checkout.
- **Search-engine improvements**: better language tags and structured data so your store ranks for both Arabic and English searches.

### Portal & POS

- **Product import from CSV / Excel**: bring your whole catalog in one upload with validation, progress tracking, and a ready-made template. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/importing-products)
- **Barcode label sheets**: print A4 sheets of scannable barcode labels for any product variant.
- **WhatsApp receipts & order updates**: send digital receipts and status updates to customers in one tap. [Learn more](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing)
- **Store QR poster**: print a "scan to order" poster with your store QR code for the counter or window.
- **Create purchase order from low stock**: turn low-stock alerts into a pre-filled purchase order with one click.
- **Item-targeted coupons fixed**: coupons limited to specific products or categories now apply correctly at checkout.
- **Arabic notifications**: in-app notifications now match your portal language.
