## July 2026

### Online store

- **WhatsApp chat button**: a floating WhatsApp button on every storefront page, with a dedicated WhatsApp number and an on/off toggle in Social Media Links. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/social-whatsapp-button)
- **Smarter order tracking**: the phone field prefills right after checkout, each status explains what happens next, and customers can WhatsApp or call you about their order. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/order-tracking)
- **Order tracking page**: customers can now follow their order status at `/track` on your storefront using the order number and phone. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/order-tracking)
- **Coupon codes at checkout**: shoppers can apply your coupons directly in the online checkout and see the discount before placing the order. [Learn more](/docs/marketing/apply-coupons)
- **Review stars on product cards**: average ratings now show on product listings, not just product pages. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/product-reviews)
- **Recently-viewed products**: a strip on your storefront helps shoppers pick up where they left off. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview)
- **Your brand color everywhere**: set a primary color for your storefront so it stops looking like everyone else's. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-appearance)
- **Minimum-order progress**: carts show "add X more to place your order" instead of a surprise at checkout. [Learn more](/docs/stores/storefront-payment-methods)
- **Search-engine improvements**: better language tags and structured data so your store ranks for both Arabic and English searches. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/storefront-seo-content)

### Portal & POS

- **Scan-to-create at the register**: scanning a barcode that isn't in your catalogue opens **Quick Add Product** with the code prefilled as the SKU. Create it and it drops straight into the sale. Auto-generated SKUs now keep Arabic product names readable too. [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-barcode-scanning)
- **Today at this store**: from New Sale, open today's revenue, orders, and cash vs non-cash for the store you are on. [Learn more](/docs/orders/pos-daily-summary)
- **Dead stock report**: see how much cash is sleeping on your shelves with 30 / 60 / 90 day windows. [Learn more](/docs/reports/dead-stock-analytics)
- **Daily and weekly sales digests**: evening push with net sales and profit (screenshot-ready). [Learn more](/docs/settings/sales-digest-notifications)
- **App icon badge**: installed apps show unread notification count on the home-screen icon. [Learn more](/docs/settings/account-settings#installing-the-app)
- **Native share for WhatsApp**: phones open the system share sheet before falling back to WhatsApp links. [Learn more](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing)
- **Product import from CSV / Excel**: bring your whole catalog in one upload with validation, progress tracking, and a ready-made template. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/importing-products)
- **Barcode label sheets**: print A4 sheets of scannable barcode labels for any product variant. [Learn more](/docs/catalogue/barcode-labels)
- **WhatsApp receipts & order updates**: send digital receipts and status updates to customers in one tap. [Learn more](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing)
- **Store QR poster**: print a "scan to order" poster with your store QR code for the counter or window. [Learn more](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store)
- **Create purchase order from low stock**: turn low-stock alerts into a pre-filled purchase order with one click. [Learn more](/docs/inventory/low-stock-alerts)
- **Item-targeted coupons fixed**: coupons limited to specific products or categories now apply correctly at checkout. [Learn more](/docs/marketing/coupon-rules)
- **Arabic notifications**: in-app notifications now match your portal language.
