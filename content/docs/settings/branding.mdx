---

## Accessing Branding Settings

1. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Open **Branding**.

<Callout title="Note">
The Branding page is visible to business-level users with the branding permission.
</Callout>

## Uploading Your Logo

The branding setting is your **business logo**:

1. Click the upload area and choose your logo file.
2. Supported formats: **PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP** (up to 5 MB).
3. The logo is saved automatically after upload.

## Where Your Logo Appears

- **Receipts** — printed and digital receipts show your logo when **Show Logo** is enabled in [Receipt Settings](/docs/stores/receipt-settings).
- **[Online Store](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview)** — note that the storefront has its **own separate logo**, uploaded in the Online Store settings for each store.

<Callout title="Tip">
Use a clear, high-quality logo that looks good at small sizes. PNG with a transparent background works best on receipts.
</Callout>
