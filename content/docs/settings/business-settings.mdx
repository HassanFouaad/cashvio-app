## Business Information

Go to **Settings > Business** to view your business details:

- **Business Name**
- **Contact Phone** (and a secondary phone, if set)

<Callout title="Note">
Business information is currently **view-only** in the portal. It was set when your account was created — contact [support](/contact) if you need to change it.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Who can see this?">
The **Business**, **Branding**, and **Plan** pages are visible only to business-level users (like the account owner). Team members with store-level roles do not see them.
</Callout>

## Plan & Features

Go to **Settings > Plan** to see everything included in your plan. Features are grouped into three sections:

| Group | What it contains |
|-------|------------------|
| **Modules** | The main sections of the system (Orders, Products, Inventory, Customers, Suppliers, Analytics, POS, Roles, Online Store, Coupons, Overdue Payments) |
| **Advanced Features** | Extra capabilities such as advanced analytics, advanced inventory, and custom reports |
| **Capacity Limits** | Your plan's limits — maximum stores, users, and POS devices ("Unlimited" when there is no cap) |

### Turning features on and off

Features you are allowed to control show a **toggle switch** — for example, you can switch off a module your team doesn't use, and it disappears from the sidebar for everyone.

Some features display a chip instead of a toggle:

| Chip | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Upgrade** | Not included in your current plan — upgrade to unlock it |
| **Locked** | Included and always on in your plan — cannot be switched off |
| **Custom** | Set specifically for your account by the Cashvio team |

<Callout title="Tip">
Turning off unused modules keeps the portal simpler for your team. You can switch them back on at any time without losing data.
</Callout>
