---
title: Daily and Weekly Sales Digests
description: "Get an evening push with today's (or this week's) sales and profit so you can screenshot the win and share it."
---

Cashvio can send you a short **sales digest** push notification after the trading day. The message is built to be screenshot-friendly: revenue and profit in one line.

## What you get

**Daily digest** (around **9:00 PM** Egypt time):

> You sold EGP X today, profit EGP Y

**Weekly digest** (Sunday around **9:00 PM** Egypt time):

> You sold EGP X this week, profit EGP Y

Amounts use **completed orders only**, with **net revenue** (after refunds) and profit after cost, matching how your analytics reports think about money.

## Who receives it

Active users on the tenant get the digest when there was at least one completed sale in the window. It appears as a normal push and in the in-app notification feed (same place as the bell).

## How to see it on your phone

1. [Install Cashvio as an app](/docs/settings/account-settings#installing-the-app) and allow notifications when asked.
2. Keep the app signed in so your device stays registered.
3. When the digest arrives, open it or screenshot the notification card to share with a partner or on WhatsApp.

<Callout title="Tip">
The digest is one of the easiest "we sold this today" moments to share. Pair it with [WhatsApp Sharing](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing) when you want to send a receipt to a customer instead.
</Callout>

## FAQs

**Q: Can I change the 9:00 PM time?**
A: Not yet. Digests use Egypt evening time so most shops get them after close of day.

**Q: I got no digest today. Why?**
A: Either there were no completed sales in the window, notifications are blocked on the device, or the app is not installed / not registered for push.
