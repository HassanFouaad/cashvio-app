## Accessing Account Settings

Go to **Settings > Account** in the sidebar, or click the account icon at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My Profile**. Every user has their own account page.

The page has five sections: **Profile**, **Sign-in & security**, **Push notifications**, **In-app sounds**, and **Install App**.

## Profile

Your **name, username, and role** are shown here. Name changes are made by an admin from the [user's details page](/docs/team/users).

## Sign-in & security

This section shows the **email** you sign in with, your **password**, and your **Google** connection. Each has its own button, and each change opens in a window.

### Changing Your Email

1. Click **Change Email** next to your email.
2. Enter your **new email address** and your **current password**, then click **Send Verification**. Cashvio asks for your password to make sure the request really comes from you.
3. A **verification email** is sent to the new address.
4. Open the link in that email and click **Confirm Email Change** to complete the change.

The email only changes after you confirm it from the verification link. Until then, your old email keeps working.

<Callout title="After you confirm the new email">
Cashvio signs you out on every device, including the one you used to confirm. Sign in again with your **new email**. Cashvio also sends a security notice to your **old email address**. If you get that notice and did not make the change, reset your password and contact support right away.
</Callout>

### Changing Your Password

1. Click **Change Password** next to your password.
2. Enter your **current password**.
3. Enter your **new password** (at least 8 characters, different from the current one) and confirm it.
4. Click **Change Password**.

Changing your password signs you out everywhere, including this device. Sign in again with your new password on each device you use.

If you close either window after typing, Cashvio asks before discarding what you entered.

After several wrong current passwords in a row, whether you are changing your password or your email, Cashvio pauses both changes for your account for a short time and tells you how long to wait. You stay signed in while you wait.

### Google

The **Google** row shows whether you can sign in with **Continue with Google**:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Connected as …** | Followed by the Google email. You can sign in with that Google account. |
| **Not connected** | You sign in with your email or username and your password. |

To connect Google:

1. Click **Connect Google**.
2. Enter your **current password**.
3. Choose your Google account in the Google window.

To disconnect Google:

1. Click **Disconnect**.
2. Enter your **current password**.

**Disconnect** needs a password on your account, so you can still sign in afterwards. If you signed up with Google, set a password first. Disconnecting doesn't sign you out anywhere: your other devices stay signed in.

Wrong current passwords here count toward the same short pause as password and email changes.

<Callout title="Security emails">
Cashvio sends you a security email whenever Google is connected to or disconnected from your account. If you get one and did not make the change, reset your password and contact support right away.
</Callout>

If an admin changes your account email, Google is disconnected and you are signed out on every device. Sign in with your password (or set one with **Forgot password?** on the login page), then connect Google again.

### Set a password

If you signed up with Google, your account has no password yet. **Set a password** appears in place of **Change Email** and **Change Password**.

1. Click **Set a password**. Cashvio emails you a link to choose a password, the same link **Forgot password?** sends from the login page.
2. Open the email, then enter and confirm your new password (at least 8 characters).

After that, you can sign in with your email and password or with Google, and **Change Email**, **Change Password**, and **Disconnect** become available.

## Push notifications

**This device** shows whether the device you are using gets order and inventory alerts:

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Receiving** | This device gets notifications. | Nothing. |
| **Not enabled** | You have not allowed notifications yet. | Click **Enable notifications** and allow them in the browser prompt. |
| **Blocked** | Notifications are blocked for this site. | Allow them in your browser settings, then reload the page. |
| **Not receiving** | Notifications are allowed, but this device is not registered. | Click **Re-register this device**. |
| **Not supported** | This browser cannot receive notifications. | On iPhone or iPad, add the app to your home screen first, then enable notifications from inside the installed app. |

**Registered devices** lists every device that gets your notifications, with when each was last active. The one you are using is marked **This device**. Click **Remove** to stop notifications on a device until it registers again.

## In-app sounds

Short alert tones for kitchen tickets and notifications, played in the browser on this device:

- **Enable in-app sounds**: turn all tones on or off for this device.
- **Volume**: how loud the tones are.
- **Play sound for push while this tab is focused**: use a soft in-app tone instead of the system notification sound while the portal is open.
- **Alert tones**: pick a tone for each type of alert. Click the speaker button next to a tone to hear it.

These choices are saved on this device only.

## Installing the App

The **Install App** section lets you install Cashvio as an app for faster access. Click **Install** when your browser supports it; if the app is already installed, the section shows **Installed**. On mobile, the Dashboard shows an install banner.

### App icon badge

When Cashvio is **installed** on a phone that supports app badges (most modern Android browsers, and some desktops), the home-screen icon shows a number matching your **unread notifications** in the bell. The badge clears when you mark notifications as read, or when you leave the signed-in app.

<Callout title="Note">
The badge needs an installed app (or a browser that supports Badging). In a normal browser tab with no install, you still see the count on the notification bell inside the portal.
</Callout>

## Language and Theme

Language and theme are not on this page. On a computer, use the icons at the bottom of the sidebar; on a phone, use the account menu in the top bar:

- **Language**: the translate icon switches between English and Arabic instantly.
- **Theme**: the sun/moon icon toggles between light and dark mode.

<Callout title="Note">
These preferences are personal; your language and theme choices don't affect other team members.
</Callout>
