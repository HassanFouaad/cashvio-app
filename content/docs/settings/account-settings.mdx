## Accessing Account Settings

Go to **Settings > Account** in the sidebar. Every user has their own account page.

## Personal Information

Your **first name, last name, username, email, and role** are displayed here. Name changes are made by an admin from the [user's details page](/docs/team/users); what you can change yourself is your **email**:

### Changing Your Email

1. Click the **edit icon** next to your email.
2. Enter your **new email address**.
3. A **verification email** is sent to the new address.
4. Open the link in that email and click **Confirm** to complete the change.

The email only changes after you confirm it from the verification link. Until then, your old email keeps working.

## Changing Your Password

In the **Security** section:

1. Enter your **current password**.
2. Enter your **new password** (at least 8 characters, different from the current one) and confirm it.
3. Click **Save**.

## Installing the App

The Account page also has an **Install App** section, where you can install Cashvio as an app on your desktop for faster access. On mobile, the Dashboard shows an install banner.

### App icon badge

When Cashvio is **installed** on a phone that supports app badges (most modern Android browsers, and some desktops), the home-screen icon shows a number matching your **unread notifications** in the bell. The badge clears when you mark notifications as read, or when you leave the signed-in app.

<Callout title="Note">
The badge needs an installed app (or a browser that supports Badging). In a normal browser tab with no install, you still see the count on the notification bell inside the portal.
</Callout>

## Language and Theme

Language and theme are switched from the **top bar**, not from settings:

- **Language**: the translate icon switches between English and Arabic instantly.
- **Theme**: the profile menu toggles between light and dark mode.

<Callout title="Note">
These preferences are personal; your language and theme choices don't affect other team members.
</Callout>
