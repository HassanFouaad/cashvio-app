## Opening the Sale Screen

There are several ways to start a sale:

- Click the **New Sale** button, which is always pinned in the sidebar.
- Use the **New Sale** quick action on the Dashboard.
- On mobile, tap the round **New Sale** button in the center of the bottom bar.
- If you installed Cashvio as an app, long-press the app icon and choose **New Sale** to jump straight to the register.

The sale screen opens in **full-screen mode**, designed for fast, touch-friendly selling at the counter.

<Callout title="Note">
If you have multiple stores, you will be asked to **choose the store** first. The catalogue, prices, and payment methods all follow the selected store.
</Callout>

From the top bar you can also open **Today at this store** for a quick look at today's revenue, orders, and cash vs non-cash for the store you are selling from. See [Today at This Store](/docs/orders/pos-daily-summary).

## The Catalogue Panel

The main area of the screen shows your products as **tiles**:

1. **Browse by category**: Use the category bar to filter products.
2. **Search**: Find products by name using the search field.
3. **Tap a product** to add it to the cart. If the product has multiple variants (size, color, etc.), a variant picker opens so you can choose the right one.
4. Out-of-stock products are indicated on the tile, and stock limits are enforced when adding to the cart.

To add items with a USB or Bluetooth scanner or your device camera, see [Barcode Scanning on New Sale](/docs/orders/pos-barcode-scanning).

## The Cart (Ticket)

The cart panel shows everything about the current sale: customer, fulfillment method, line items, and the running total. On **mobile**, the cart appears as a bar at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to open the full cart drawer.

From the cart you can attach a customer, apply coupons and manual discounts, add service or delivery fees, and sell custom items that are not in your catalogue. See [Cart, Discounts, and Fees](/docs/orders/pos-cart-discounts) for the full walkthrough.

## Taking Payment

When the cart is ready, tap **Charge / Checkout** to open the payment dialog. Choose cash, online, receipt, or store credit, take partial or scheduled payments, or skip payment for pickup and delivery orders that will be paid later.

See [Taking Payment and After the Sale](/docs/orders/pos-taking-payment) for payment methods, store credit, partial payments, and what happens on the success screen.
