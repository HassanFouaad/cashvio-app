title: Creating Orders (New Sale)
description: Step-by-step guide to the full-screen sale screen — adding products, applying coupons and discounts, and taking payment.
---

## Opening the Sale Screen

There are two ways to start a sale:

- Click the **New Sale** button — it is always pinned in the sidebar.
- Use the **New Sale** quick action on the Dashboard.

The sale screen opens in **full-screen mode**, designed for fast, touch-friendly selling at the counter.

<Callout title="Note">
If you have multiple stores, you will be asked to **choose the store** first. The catalogue, prices, and payment methods all follow the selected store.
</Callout>

## The Catalogue Panel

The main area of the screen shows your products as **tiles**:

1. **Browse by category** — Use the category bar to filter products.
2. **Search** — Find products by name using the search field.
3. **Tap a product** to add it to the cart. If the product has multiple variants (size, color, etc.), a variant picker opens so you can choose the right one.
4. Out-of-stock products are indicated on the tile, and stock limits are enforced when adding to the cart.

### Custom Items

To sell something that is not in your catalogue (a service or a one-off item), open the cart menu and choose **Custom Item**:

- Enter a name, price, and quantity.
- Custom items do not affect inventory levels.

## The Cart (Ticket)

The cart panel shows everything about the current sale:

| Section | What you can do |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Customer** | Attach an existing customer or create a new one on the spot. The customer's store credit balance is shown. |
| **Fulfillment** | Choose In-Store, Pickup, Delivery, or Dine-In. Delivery asks for the customer's address. |
| **Items** | Adjust quantities with + / −, or remove lines. |
| **Summary** | Subtotal, fees, discount, coupon, tax, and the final total. |

On **mobile**, the cart appears as a bar at the bottom of the screen — tap it to open the full cart drawer.

### Coupons

1. Open the **Coupon** option in the cart.
2. Enter the coupon code.
3. The code is validated instantly — active status, validity dates, usage limits, and minimum order value are all checked — and the discount is applied to the summary.

See [Coupons](/docs/marketing/coupons) for how to create and manage coupon codes.

### Manual Discounts

1. Open the **Discount** option in the cart.
2. Choose **Fixed Amount** (e.g., 50 EGP off) or **Percentage** (e.g., 10% off).
3. The total updates automatically.

### Fees and Notes

- **[Service fees](/docs/stores/service-fees)** and **[delivery fees](/docs/stores/delivery-zones)** are added automatically based on your store configuration, and can be edited on the sale.
- Add an optional **note** to the order from the cart menu.

## Taking Payment

Click **Charge / Checkout** to open the payment dialog.

### Store Credit

If the attached customer has store credit:

- A **Use Store Credit** toggle appears at the top.
- Enable it to apply the balance toward the order. If credit covers the whole amount, no other payment method is needed.

### Payment Methods

Choose the method for the remaining amount:

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Quick-amount buttons let you tap the tendered cash in one touch; the **change due** is calculated automatically. |
| **Online** | Digital payment (card, InstaPay, mobile wallet, bank transfer, etc.). |
| **Receipt** | Transfer payment with an optional receipt image upload (JPEG, PNG, or WebP). |

<Callout title="Note">
Only payment methods **enabled** for the selected store appear. See [Payment Methods](/docs/stores/payment-methods) to configure them.
</Callout>

### Partial and Scheduled Payments

You can take **part of the total now** and schedule the rest:

1. Edit the **amount to pay now**.
2. Enable **Schedule Remaining Payment** and pick a **due date**.
3. The remainder becomes a scheduled payment you can track in [Overdue Payments](/docs/orders/overdue-payments).

### Pay Later

For **Pickup, Delivery, and Dine-In** orders, you can skip payment entirely and collect it later — the order is created as unpaid. In-Store sales always require payment.

## After the Sale

The **success screen** shows:

- The **change due** (large and clear, when the customer paid cash)
- The order number and total
- **New Sale** — start the next sale immediately
- **Print Receipt** — print via a connected thermal printer or the browser print dialog. If **auto-print** is enabled in your [receipt settings](/docs/stores/receipt-settings), the receipt prints automatically.
- **View Orders** — exit to the orders list

The order appears in **Sales** with its order number, inventory is adjusted automatically (when tracking is enabled), and the order source is recorded for analytics.

<Callout title="Tip">
Need a digital receipt? Open the order from the **Sales** list — from the order details you can download a **PDF**, copy a **shareable receipt link**, or show a **QR code** the customer can scan.
</Callout>
