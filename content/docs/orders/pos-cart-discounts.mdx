The cart panel on **New Sale** shows everything about the current sale:

| Section | What you can do |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Customer** | Attach an existing customer or create a new one on the spot. The customer's store credit balance is shown. |
| **Fulfillment** | Choose In-Store, Pickup, Delivery, or Dine-In (when Cafe/Restaurant Mode is on). Delivery asks for the customer's address. Dine-In can include an optional **table number**. |
| **Items** | Adjust quantities with + / −, or tap a line for **Change extras**, **Duplicate**, **Remove** and an **item note** for the kitchen. **Clear** empties the ticket, and **Undo** brings back what you removed (see [Change a line](/docs/orders/creating-orders#change-a-line)). Lines stay compact so you see many at once. |
| **Summary** | Subtotal, fees, discount, coupon, tax, and the final total, always visible above **Charge**. |

On **mobile**, the cart appears as a bar at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to open the full cart drawer.

## Table Number (Dine-In)

<Callout title="Cafe/Restaurant Mode">
**Dine-In** and table numbers appear only after you enable **Cafe/Restaurant Mode** in **Settings > Features** (or Feature selection). Off by default.
</Callout>

When fulfillment is **Dine-In**, the cart shows an optional **Table** row (same pattern as the delivery address row):

1. Tap the row and enter the table number or label (up to 50 characters, for example `12` or `A1`), or leave it empty.
2. Save. The table stays on the ticket until you change fulfillment.
3. **Charge** works with or without a table number. You can set or edit it later on [Order Details](/docs/orders/managing-orders).

When set, the table number is saved on the order and shown on the [Kitchen board](/docs/orders/kitchen-board) so the kitchen and runners can match tickets to tables.

Switching away from Dine-In clears the table number from the cart.

## Coupons

1. Open the **Coupon** option in the cart.
2. Enter the coupon code.
3. The code is validated instantly (active status, validity dates, usage limits, and minimum order value are all checked) and the discount is applied to the summary.

See [Coupons](/docs/marketing/coupons) for how to create and manage coupon codes.

## Manual Discounts

1. Open the **Discount** option in the cart.
2. Choose **Fixed Amount** (e.g., 50 EGP off) or **Percentage** (e.g., 10% off). For percentages, tap one of the quick chips (5%, 10%, 15%, 20%) or type any value.
3. The dialog previews the discount and the amount **after discount**; the ticket total updates when you tap **Apply Discount**.

**The dialog remembers your last discount.** On a ticket that has no discount yet, the dialog opens on the last discount you applied in this store (same type and amount, with the matching percentage chip highlighted), even after **New Sale** or a reload. It is only prefilled: nothing changes on the ticket until you tap **Apply Discount**.

- A discount already on the ticket always wins: the dialog opens on that one.
- Cancelling the dialog does not change what is remembered.
- **Remove Discount** clears the discount from the ticket only; the dialog still opens on it next time.
- A remembered fixed amount larger than the current subtotal is reduced to the subtotal.

See [Favorites, Recent, and Remembered Defaults](/docs/orders/pos-remembered-defaults) for everything else the register remembers.

## Item Notes

Use **item notes** for kitchen instructions on a single line (for example no sugar, extra shot, or no onions):

1. Tap the cart line (its name or price). The line sheet opens with the cursor in **Note**.
2. Type the instruction (up to 500 characters) and tap **Save**.
3. The note appears under that line on the ticket and the small note mark turns green. Tap the line again to edit or clear it.

To give one unit its own note, tap **Duplicate** in the same sheet: the new line starts without a note.

Item notes print on receipts and show on [Kitchen](/docs/orders/kitchen-board) tickets so the kitchen sees them with the item. They are separate from the optional **order note** below.

## Fees and Notes

- **[Service fees](/docs/stores/service-fees)** and **[delivery fees](/docs/stores/delivery-zones)** are added automatically based on your store configuration, and can be edited on the sale.
- Add an optional **note** to the whole order with the **Note** button under the lines, next to **Discount**, **Coupon** and **Hold** (general comments, not per-item kitchen mods).

## Custom Items

To sell something that is not in your catalogue (a service or a one-off item), open the cart menu and choose **Custom Item**:

- Enter a name, price, and quantity.
- Custom items do not affect inventory levels.
