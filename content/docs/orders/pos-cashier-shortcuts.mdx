Small counter habits that save time on every sale. These work on the full-screen **New Sale** screen and in store **Receipt** settings.

## Type a quantity (bulk packs)

The ticket stepper is fine for 1 or 2 units. For a case of 24 bottles, tapping **+** twenty-four times is the wrong tool.

1. Open **New Sale** and add the product to the ticket.
2. Tap the **quantity number** between the minus and plus controls.
3. Type the count (for example `24`) and press Enter, or tap outside the field.
4. **Max quantity per order** and available stock still apply. If you type above either limit, you see an error and the line stays unchanged (same rules as the plus button).
5. Type `0` to remove the line.

Plus and minus still work for small adjustments.

## Search with the keyboard

With a keyboard you can find and add products without touching the screen. Click into the search field (it is focused when **New Sale** opens), then:

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| **↓ / ↑** | Move a highlight through the products shown, in the order you see them. The list scrolls to keep the highlight visible. Works in **All**, **Favorites** and **Recent**, with or without a search term. |
| **Enter** | Adds the highlighted product, with the same checks as a tap (the option picker or add-ons dialog opens when the product needs them). With nothing highlighted, Enter works as before: an exact barcode or SKU, then a single clear name match. |
| **Escape** | Clears the highlight first, then the search text, then an armed quantity multiplier. |

Typing anything removes the highlight, so a barcode scanner (which types the code and presses Enter) keeps working exactly as before.

## Set the quantity before adding (`n*`)

Type `n*` in the search field, for example `12*`, to add the next product 12 times. A **×12** chip appears in the search field; scan or tap the product once and the chip clears. Any number from 2 to 999 works, and stock and **Max quantity per order** still apply. You can also tap the **×** button (**Add several**) in the catalogue toolbar. See [Add several units at once](/docs/orders/pos-barcode-scanning#add-several-units-at-once) for the details.

## Type a price to ring up a custom item

For an item that is not in your catalogue, type its price in the search field and press Enter, for example `35` or `12.50`.

1. The register first checks for a product with that barcode or SKU, or a clear name match. If one exists, it is added as usual.
2. If nothing matches, the **Custom Item** dialog opens with **Item Price** already filled in and the cursor in **Item Name**.
3. Type the name, check the quantity, and tap **Add to Cart**.

Only plain prices count: up to six digits before the decimal point and up to two after it (Arabic digits and the Arabic decimal separator also work). A number with leading zeros (such as `0012`), a longer number, or a scanned code is treated as a barcode, so unknown barcodes still open **Quick Add Product** as before.

## Open the cash drawer (no sale)

Use this when you need the drawer open without completing a sale (change, float check, or end-of-day).

1. On **New Sale**, tap **Drawer** in the top bar (on phones it is the cash icon, **Open cash drawer**).
2. Tap it. The paired thermal printer sends a drawer-kick pulse (same signal used after a cash sale print).
3. If no printer is paired yet, the browser asks you to choose one first.

<Callout title="Needs a thermal printer">
The drawer opens through the receipt printer's kick cable. Browser / system print cannot open a drawer. Pair the printer under **Settings > Stores > Receipt** first if the icon is missing or pairing fails.
</Callout>

In stores that use [cash sessions](/docs/orders/cash-sessions), the cash icon first asks for a reason and logs the no-sale on the open register, then opens the drawer. Without a paired printer the no-sale is still logged and you open the drawer by hand.

You can also open the drawer from **Receipt** settings (see below).

## Pair and test the printer before a sale

Pairing used to happen mid-sale while a customer waited. Do it once in settings:

1. Go to **Settings > Stores**, open your store, then the **Receipt** tab.
2. In the **Printer** card, check the status line (paired device name, browser print, or none).
3. Tap **Pair printer** (or **Change printer**) and pick the USB or Bluetooth COM device.
4. Tap **Test print** to send a sample receipt labeled `TEST-PRINT` (not a real order).
5. Optionally tap **Open cash drawer** to confirm the kick cable.

Your choice is remembered **per store on this browser**. Use **Remove** if you need to clear it.

## FAQs

**Q: Does typing a quantity skip stock checks?**
A: No. The same stock and max-per-order rules as the plus button still apply.

**Q: Why is Open cash drawer missing?**
A: The icon appears only when this browser supports thermal printing (Chrome or Edge with Web Serial or WebUSB). Pair and test from **Receipt** settings on a supported device.

**Q: Will Test print create an order in reports?**
A: No. It prints a local sample with order number `TEST-PRINT` and never calls the API.
