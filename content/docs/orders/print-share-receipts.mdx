title: Print and Share Receipts
description: "Print or reprint a receipt, share it by link, WhatsApp or QR, and follow up on print jobs that need attention."
---

Open the order details and click **Print / Share** in the header:

- **Print**: sends the receipt to your paired thermal printer, or opens the browser print dialog if that is your chosen method (remembered per store)
- **PDF**: opens the browser print dialog, where you can save the receipt as a PDF
- **Share Link**: copies a public digital-receipt link the customer can open
- **Send via WhatsApp**: opens WhatsApp with the receipt link ready to send to the customer (see [WhatsApp Sharing](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing))
- **QR Code**: shows a QR code the customer can scan to view their receipt

A reprint prints the current order data and your current [Receipt Settings](/docs/stores/receipt-settings) with the original order date, and is labeled **COPY - Current order** on the paper so it cannot be confused with the first print. Printing never opens the cash drawer.

## Print jobs

Every print, pairing, connection check and drawer command is listed under a **Print jobs** button that stays in the corner of the screen — including after you start a new sale or leave the page. If any job failed or ended unclearly, the button turns into **Print jobs need attention**.

The app tells you what it actually observed — it never claims paper came out:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **In progress** | The app is preparing or sending the job |
| **Command completed** | The command was fully sent to the printer. Check the printer for paper output — this does not itself confirm the customer received the receipt |
| **Print dialog opened** | The browser print dialog opened; finish the job there |
| **Failed** | The job did not reach the printer. The reason is shown under the status |
| **Output uncertain** | The printer may have received part of the job. Check the paper before reprinting, so you do not hand the customer two copies |
| **Cancelled** | The printer selection was cancelled. Your saved printer has not changed |

<Callout title="If a receipt did not print">
Do not create another sale or payment — the sale itself is complete. Check the printer and paper first, then open the order from **Sales**, click **Print / Share** and print a copy. Use the job list to confirm what happened to the first attempt.
</Callout>

Each job shows **Close** to clear it from the list, and receipt jobs also link to the order details.

The receipt follows your [Receipt Settings](/docs/stores/receipt-settings), including the auto-print option.

## Digital receipt page

When a customer opens the public receipt link (or scans the QR on a thermal print), they see a digital receipt hosted on Cashvio. At the bottom there is a **Get Cashvio free** link for shop owners who want the same free POS and online store. That link is for merchants, not for changing the customer receipt itself.

## Notifying Customers on WhatsApp

When an order has a customer phone number, **WhatsApp customer** appears under **Other actions** in the order details header. It opens WhatsApp with a message matching the order's current fulfillment status (received, preparing, ready, delivering, completed, or cancelled), plus a link to the order.

See [WhatsApp Sharing & Customer Updates](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing) for the full flow and message templates.
