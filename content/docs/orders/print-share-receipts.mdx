---
title: Print and Share Receipts
description: "Print, download, link, WhatsApp, or QR-share a receipt from order details, following your store receipt settings."
---

Open the order details and click **Print / Share Receipt**:

- **Print**: via a paired thermal printer or the browser print dialog (your choice is remembered per store)
- **Download PDF**: save the receipt as a PDF
- **Share Link**: copy a public digital-receipt link the customer can open
- **Send via WhatsApp**: open WhatsApp with the receipt link ready to send to the customer (see [WhatsApp Sharing](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing))
- **QR Code**: show a QR code the customer can scan to view their receipt

The receipt follows your [Receipt Settings](/docs/stores/receipt-settings), including the auto-print option.

## Notifying Customers on WhatsApp

When an order has a customer phone number, a **WhatsApp customer** button appears in the order details header. It opens WhatsApp with a message matching the order's current fulfillment status (received, preparing, ready, delivering, completed, or cancelled), plus a link to the order.

See [WhatsApp Sharing & Customer Updates](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing) for the full flow and message templates.
