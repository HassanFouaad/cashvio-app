title: Today at This Store (POS Summary)
description: "See today's revenue, orders, and cash vs non-cash totals for the store you are selling from, without leaving New Sale."
---

While you are on the full-screen **New Sale** screen, you can open a quick day summary for the store you selected. Think of it as a lightweight X-report. To count a drawer and print a Z report, use [cash sessions](/docs/orders/cash-sessions).

## Open the summary

1. Start a sale from **New Sale** (or open an existing sale screen).
2. In the top bar, tap **Today** (on phones it is the reports icon, next to **Exit**).
3. A drawer opens with today's numbers for that store.

On phones the drawer slides up from the bottom; on desktop it opens from the side.

<Callout title="Who can see it">
The button appears only if your plan includes analytics and your role can view orders. If you do not see the icon, ask an admin to check your permissions.
</Callout>

## What you see

| Figure | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Revenue** | Net sales for completed orders at this store today |
| **Orders** | How many completed orders today |
| **Cash** | Revenue paid with cash |
| **Non-cash** | Everything else (online, receipt, store credit, and similar methods) |

Below the headline numbers, a **By payment method** list breaks down today's sales by how customers paid.

## How "today" is calculated

- The window is **today in your device timezone**, from the start of the day until now.
- Only the **store selected on the sale screen** is included (not every store in the business).
- Figures refresh when you reopen the drawer.

## FAQs

**Q: Does this replace a Z report?**
A: No. It is a quick mid-day (or end-of-day) glance at the counter based on completed orders. To count the drawer, explain differences and print a Z report, turn on [cash sessions](/docs/orders/cash-sessions).

**Q: Why are my numbers different from Reports?**
A: This drawer is scoped to **one store** and **today so far**. The Reports screens can cover other stores and longer periods.
