## Adding a Payment

If an order has an outstanding balance:

1. Open the order details.
2. Click **Add Payment**.
3. Select the payment method (Cash, Online, or Receipt).
4. If **Receipt** is selected, you can optionally upload a receipt image.
5. Enter the amount.
6. Confirm the payment.

The payment status updates automatically based on the remaining balance.

## Accepting Storefront Pending Payments

When a customer places an order through your online storefront with **Cash** or **Receipt**, the order is created with a **pending** payment. For Receipt, the customer also uploads a receipt image at checkout.

To accept the payment after you receive the cash (or verify the receipt):

1. Open the order details. You will see the **Accept Payment** button in the header (instead of the usual **Add Payment** button).
2. Click **Accept Payment**.
3. Review the confirmation. For Receipt payments, the uploaded receipt image is shown in the dialog.
4. Click **Accept & Complete Payment** to mark the payment as **Completed**.

You can also approve from the **Order Payments** section on the same page.

<Callout title="Note">
**Accept Payment** appears only for storefront orders with a pending Cash or Receipt payment. Online (gateway) payments are confirmed automatically. For other orders, the regular **Add Payment** button is shown.
</Callout>
