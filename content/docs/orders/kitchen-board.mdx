The **Kitchen** board is a full-screen view for the kitchen or expo station. It shows open tickets with line items, refreshes on its own, and lets staff move each order to the next fulfillment status without opening the full order page.

<Callout title="Enable Cafe/Restaurant Mode first">
Kitchen, Dine-In, and table numbers sit behind **Cafe/Restaurant Mode**, which is off by default. Turn it on in **Settings > Features** (or during Feature selection when you set up your account). Until then, **Sales > Kitchen** does not appear.
</Callout>

## Open the board

1. Go to **Sales > Kitchen** in the sidebar (or open the **Kitchen** tab under Sales).
2. If you have more than one store, pick the store you want to watch.
3. Leave the page open on a tablet or second screen near the kitchen.

<Callout title="Who can use it">
You need permission to **view orders** to open the board. Advancing a ticket needs permission to **edit orders**. If the page or the advance button is missing, ask an admin to update your role.
</Callout>

## What each ticket shows

Tickets are grouped by fulfillment status, typically **Preparing** and **Ready**. Each card includes:

| Detail | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Order number** | Match the ticket to the POS or online order |
| **Table number** | Shown for dine-in orders when set, so runners know where to deliver |
| **Fulfillment method** | Dine-In, Pickup, or Delivery |
| **Elapsed time** | How long the ticket has been waiting (color changes as it ages; see below) |
| **Line items** | Quantity and product name for every item |
| **Item notes** | Kitchen instructions under a line (for example no sugar), when the cashier added them on [New Sale](/docs/orders/pos-cart-discounts) |
| **Order notes** | Whole-order comments, when set |

In-store walk-up sales do not appear here. Those complete at the counter with no kitchen tracking.

## Elapsed time colors

Each ticket shows how long it has been open since it was created. The elapsed badge changes color so slow tickets stand out at a glance:

| Stage | When | Color |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Fresh** | Under 5 minutes | Default text |
| **Warning** | 5 minutes or more | Warning (amber) |
| **Critical** | 15 minutes or more | Error (red) |

Leave the board open so the timer keeps updating while staff cook.

## New ticket sound

When a new kitchen ticket appears on the board, Cashvio can play a short **Kitchen new ticket** alert tone in the browser.

Turn sounds on or off and pick the tone under **Settings > Account** in the **In-app sounds** section (notification sounds). Enable **In-app sounds** on the kitchen device, and leave volume up.

<Callout title="Browser audio unlock">
Most browsers block sound until someone clicks or taps the page once. After you open **Sales > Kitchen**, tap anywhere on the board (or use a settings preview) so audio can play. Keep the kitchen page open on that tablet or screen.
</Callout>

## Advance a ticket

On each card, tap the primary button to move the order to the **next** fulfillment status (for example Preparing to Ready, then Ready to Completed).

You can still jump statuses or cancel from [Order Details](/docs/orders/order-fulfillment-status) if you need a non-sequential change. The board is built for the fast path on the line.

## Refresh and polling

The board **refetches about every 5 seconds** while the page is open, so new POS and online tickets appear without a manual reload. You can also leave the page and reopen it to force a fresh load.

## Tips for cafes

- Set a **table number** when you know it (on New Sale or later on Order Details). It is optional; tickets still appear without one. See [Cart, Discounts, and Fees](/docs/orders/pos-cart-discounts).
- Add **item notes** on New Sale for mods the kitchen must see (no sugar, extra shot). They appear on the ticket under that line.
- Scope a **service fee** to dine-in only (for example 12%) under [Service Fees](/docs/stores/service-fees).
- Enable **Dine-In** for the portal (and the storefront if guests order for dine-in online) under [Fulfillment Methods](/docs/stores/fulfillment-methods). The storefront does not collect a table number.
