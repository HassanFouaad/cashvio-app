## Taking Payment

Click **Charge / Checkout** to open the payment dialog.

### Store Credit

If the attached customer has store credit:

- A **Use Store Credit** toggle appears at the top.
- Enable it to apply the balance toward the order. If credit covers the whole amount, no other payment method is needed.

### Payment Methods

Choose the method for the remaining amount:

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Enter the **Cash received** or tap a quick amount; the **Change due** is calculated automatically. See [Cash Received and Change](#cash-received-and-change). |
| **Online** | Digital payment (card, InstaPay, mobile wallet, bank transfer, etc.). |
| **Receipt** | Transfer payment with an optional receipt image upload (JPEG, PNG, or WebP). |

<Callout title="Note">
Only payment methods **enabled** for the selected store appear. See [Payment Methods](/docs/stores/payment-methods) to configure them.
</Callout>

### Cash Received and Change

When you choose **Cash**, a **Cash received** field appears, set to the amount to pay. Quick amounts sit right under it:

- **Exact** means the customer handed over the exact amount, so there is no change. It is selected when you choose **Cash**.
- Up to three suggested amounts round the total up to the next banknote of your store's currency. For example, a total of 143 in Egyptian pounds suggests 145, 150 and 160.

Tap a quick amount, or type the amount you received in **Cash received**. When it is more than the amount to pay, **Change due** shows the difference. When it is less, the dialog warns **Cash received is less than the amount to pay** and **Charge** stays unavailable until you enter enough.

| Store currency | Banknotes used |
|----------------|----------------|
| Egyptian pound (EGP) | 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200 |
| Saudi riyal (SAR) | 5, 10, 50, 100, 500 |
| UAE dirham (AED) | 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200 |
| Any other currency | 10, 50, 100, 200 |

With a keyboard, press Enter to charge. If the cursor is still in **Cash received**, the first Enter confirms the amount and the second one charges.

#### Cash Keypad on Touch Screens

On phones and tablets you use by touch (no mouse or trackpad), choosing **Cash** shows an on-screen keypad under **Cash received**, and tapping the field does not open the device keyboard. If a keyboard is connected to the device, you can still type into the field.

| Keys | What they do |
|------|--------------|
| **Exact** and the suggested amounts | Set the cash received to that amount. |
| Banknote keys (**+5**, **+10**, **+50** and so on) | Add that note to the cash received, so you can count notes as the customer hands them over. If the amount is still **Exact**, the first note starts from zero: **+100** then **+50** gives 150. |
| **1** to **9**, **0** and **00** | Type the amount. After **Exact**, a suggested amount or a banknote key, the next digit starts a new amount. |
| **.** | Starts the decimals, up to two digits. |
| Delete key (on the right) | Removes the last digit. If you delete every digit, the cash received is zero and **Charge** stays unavailable until you enter enough. Tap **Exact** to go back to the exact amount. |

The banknote keys use the notes of your store's currency from the table above. **Change due** updates with every tap. The keypad has the same layout in Arabic, with the digits in the same order.

### Partial and Scheduled Payments

You can take **part of the total now** and schedule the rest:

1. The payment screen shows the **amount to pay now** as the full total. Tap **Edit** next to it and enter the part you are taking now.
2. Enable **Schedule Remaining Payment** and pick a **due date**.
3. The remainder becomes a scheduled payment you can track in [Overdue Payments](/docs/orders/overdue-payments).

### Pay Later (Customer Credit)

When **Customer Credit (Pay Later)** is enabled under **Settings > Plan**, you can sell on credit to a selected customer:

- Click **Pay later** to skip payment now. Works for **Pickup**, **Delivery**, **Dine-In**, and **In-Store**.
- For a **partial payment**, enable **Put the rest on customer credit** instead of scheduling a separate payment row.

See [Customer Credit (Pay Later)](/docs/customers/customer-credit) for amount owed on customer profiles, filters, and collecting later.

Without the credit module, **Pickup**, **Delivery**, and **Dine-In** can still be saved unpaid at checkout; **In-Store** sales require payment unless credit is enabled.

## After the Sale

The **success screen** shows:

- The **change due** (large and clear, when the customer paid cash)
- The order number, then a short summary: total, how it was paid, item count and the customer when one was attached
- Three tiles: **Print receipt** (a connected thermal printer or the browser print dialog; with **auto-print** on in your [receipt settings](/docs/stores/receipt-settings) it prints by itself), **Send via WhatsApp** (see [WhatsApp Sharing](/docs/orders/whatsapp-sharing)) and **View orders**
- **New sale**, pinned to the bottom edge so it is always under your thumb. On a computer, Enter, Space or a barcode scan also starts the next sale.

The order appears in **Sales** with its order number, inventory is adjusted automatically (when tracking is enabled), and the order source is recorded for analytics.

<Callout title="Tip">
Need a digital receipt? Open the order from **Sales > All Sales**. From the order details you can download a **PDF**, copy a **shareable receipt link**, or show a **QR code** the customer can scan.
</Callout>
