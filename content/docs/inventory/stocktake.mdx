## What is a stocktake?

A **stocktake** is a physical count of products in a store. Scan each item (or type its SKU), set the counted quantity, then save once so Cashvio updates stock and records a **Manual Count** where the numbers differ.

Use stocktake for seasonal or periodic counts. For a one-off fix on a single item, use [Adjust Stock](/docs/inventory/adjustments-transfers) instead.

## Count with a scanner or camera

1. Go to **Inventory > Stocktake**.
2. Choose the **store** (if you have more than one).
3. Add products by scanning a barcode (USB/Bluetooth scanner or the camera button), typing a SKU and pressing Enter, or searching by **product name** and tapping a result. Each add counts as **1**. Do it again to add another.
4. Tap the number to type a full count, or use **+** and **-**.
5. You can count up to **100** products in one stocktake.

<Callout title="Tip">
No barcode? Search by product name and tap it, or type the SKU and press Enter.
</Callout>

## Print a count sheet (optional)

**Print sheet** opens an A4 worksheet with SKU, product name, system quantity, and a blank **Counted** box. Use it if you want to count on paper first, then enter the numbers in Cashvio.

## Save the count

Click **Save count**, review how many items differ from system stock, and confirm.

Cashvio applies the counts in one go:

- Stock on hand becomes the counted quantity.
- Differences are saved in [adjustment history](/docs/inventory/adjustments-transfers) as **Manual Count**.
- **Last Counted** is updated for every line you counted, even when the count matches the system.

## Stocktake vs Adjust Stock vs Consume Damaged

| Action | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Stocktake** | Physical count for many items at once |
| **Adjust Stock** | Quick correction for one item |
| **Consume / Damaged** | Stock removed as damage, loss, or waste (not a count) |
