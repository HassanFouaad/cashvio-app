If you operate multiple stores, you can transfer inventory from one store to another.

## How to Transfer Stock

1. Go to **Inventory** and open the store that currently **has** the stock (the source is the store you are viewing).
2. Find the product variant, open its **actions menu**, and select **Transfer Stock**.
3. Fill in:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Destination Store** | The store receiving the stock |
| **Quantity** | How many units to transfer (capped at the source's on-hand stock) |
| **Reason** | Optional note for the transfer |

4. Click **Confirm**.

The stock is deducted from the source store and added to the destination store instantly. Both movements appear in [adjustment history](/docs/inventory/adjustments-transfers) as **Transfer Out** and **Transfer In**.

<Callout title="Tip">
Use the Low Stock page to identify which stores need stock, then transfer from stores that have excess inventory.
</Callout>
