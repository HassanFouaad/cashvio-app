SEO settings, the announcement bar, and footer text live in the **Theme Builder**, where you edit them beside a live preview of your store. Static policy pages live under **Online Store > Pages**.

## SEO Settings

Improve your store's visibility in search engines from **Online Store > Theme > Open Theme Builder > SEO**:

- **Meta Title**: The title that appears in search engine results and browser tabs (recommended: 50-60 characters)
- **Meta Description**: A brief description of your store (recommended: 150-160 characters)
- **Favicon**: Upload a small icon (16x16 or 32x32px) that appears in browser tabs and bookmarks

Search engines are told about both English and Arabic versions of every storefront page automatically when customers share language-specific links (see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store)).

<Callout title="Tip">
Write compelling meta titles and descriptions that include relevant keywords. This helps customers find your store in search results.
</Callout>

## Announcement Bar & Footer Text

Promote offers and add a personal touch from **Online Store > Theme > Open Theme Builder > Content**:

1. Enter your **Announcement (English)** and **Announcement (Arabic)**, e.g., "Free delivery on orders over 500 EGP". The bar appears at the very top of every storefront page in your theme's primary color, in the language the customer is browsing in.
2. Enter your **Footer text (English)** and **Footer text (Arabic)**, a short message shown near the bottom of the storefront footer.
3. Click **Save Content**. The builder preview refreshes right away so you can read the new copy in place.

- If only one language is filled in, it's shown for both languages.
- Clear a field and save to **hide** the bar or footer message.

On mobile, the custom footer text also appears in the **Information** sheet from the bottom navigation bar.

<Callout title="Tip">
Announcing a real offer works best. For example, pair "Free delivery on orders over 500 EGP" with the store's [Free Delivery Threshold](/docs/stores/delivery-zones#free-delivery-threshold) so the storefront actually waives the fee automatically.
</Callout>

## Static Pages

Create informative pages for your customers from **Online Store > Pages**:

### Creating a Static Page

1. Go to **Online Store > Pages**.
2. Select the page type (Refund Policy, Shipping Policy, or About Us).
3. Use the **rich text editor** to format your content:
   - Bold and italic text
   - Bulleted and numbered lists
   - Links to other pages or websites
   - Headings and paragraphs
4. Enter content in **English** and **Arabic** for each page type.
5. Click **Save**.

These pages are automatically linked in your storefront footer and can be accessed by customers at any time.
