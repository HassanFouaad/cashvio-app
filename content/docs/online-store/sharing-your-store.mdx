## Your Store Link

Every storefront has a unique address in the format `yourstorename.cash-vio.com`:

1. Open **Online Store** from the sidebar (or **Settings > Stores > your store > Online Store**).
2. In the **Online Store URL** card, click the **copy** icon.
3. Paste the link anywhere: social media bios, WhatsApp status, Facebook page, printed flyers.

### Share in a specific language

Add `?lang=ar` or `?lang=en` to the end of any store link to open it in that language, regardless of the visitor's previous choice:

- `yourstorename.cash-vio.com?lang=ar` opens in Arabic
- `yourstorename.cash-vio.com/products?lang=en` opens the products page in English

Use the Arabic link in Arabic WhatsApp groups and ads, and the English link for English-speaking audiences. Visitors can still switch languages any time with the globe button.

## Print a QR Poster ("Scan to Order")

Turn foot traffic into online customers with a ready-to-print poster:

1. Open **Online Store > General**.
2. In the **Online Store URL** card, click **Print QR poster**.
3. Your browser's print dialog opens with an A4 poster. Print it, or choose **Save as PDF** to share it digitally.

### What's on the poster

- Your **store logo** (when uploaded in Online Store settings)
- Your **store name**
- A **"Scan to order online"** headline
- A large **QR code** pointing to your storefront
- Your store URL in plain text, for customers who prefer typing it

<Callout title="Tip">
The poster prints in your current portal language. Need both languages? Switch the portal language and print again. Many merchants display an Arabic poster at the counter and include an English one in delivery bags.
</Callout>

### Where to put it

- At the **checkout counter**, where customers scan while they wait
- On the **shop window or door**, so passers-by can browse even when you're closed
- Inside **delivery bags and packages** to turn one-time buyers into repeat online customers
- Next to your **printed receipts**, paired with the receipt QR code

<Callout title="Note">
If nothing opens when you click **Print QR poster**, allow popups for the portal in your browser and try again.
</Callout>

## Product Share Buttons

Every product page on your storefront has a **Share** row with three options:

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp with the product name and link, ready to send |
| **Facebook** | Opens the Facebook share dialog for the product page |
| **Copy link** | Copies the product URL to the clipboard |

Customers (and you!) can use these to spread your products organically. Shared links show a **rich preview** (product image, name, and description) powered by your [SEO settings](/docs/online-store/storefront-overview#seo-settings) and product images.

<Callout title="Tip">
Share your own product links on WhatsApp groups and your social pages. The link preview does the selling, so make sure your products have high-quality primary images.
</Callout>

## Track Where Customers Come From

Pair sharing with analytics to learn what works:

- Configure **Google Tag Manager, Facebook Pixel, or TikTok Pixel** in **Online Store > Tracking** (see [Integrations](/docs/integrations/google-tags))
- Watch **store visits and sales by channel** in [Reports](/docs/reports/analytics-reports)
