Connect customer-facing contact channels and control how WhatsApp appears across your storefront.

## Social Media Links & WhatsApp

Configure profiles and contact details from **Online Store > Theme > Open Theme Builder > Social**, right beside a live preview of your store:

- **Social profiles**: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and website URLs shown as icons in the storefront footer and the mobile Contact sheet
- **Contact Phone Number**: The number customers call. When empty, the store's contact phone is used instead
- **Contact Email**: Shown to customers on the storefront. When empty, the store's contact email is used instead
- **WhatsApp Number**: A dedicated number for WhatsApp chats, useful when your WhatsApp Business number differs from your call line. When empty, the contact phone number is used instead
- **Show WhatsApp Button**: Controls the floating WhatsApp chat button and all WhatsApp links on your storefront (footer icon, mobile Contact sheet, and the order-tracking help buttons). Turn it off if you don't offer WhatsApp support

## Floating WhatsApp Button

When **Show WhatsApp Button** is on, a **floating WhatsApp button** appears on every storefront page so customers can start a chat with your store in one tap.

- The button opens WhatsApp with your **WhatsApp Number** (or your contact phone when no WhatsApp number is set)
- The same number powers WhatsApp icons in the footer and mobile **Contact** sheet
- Turn the toggle off any time to hide the button and all storefront WhatsApp entry points

<Callout title="Tip">
Customers on the [order tracking page](/docs/online-store/order-tracking) get **WhatsApp the store** and **Call the store** buttons, and the WhatsApp message starts with their order number, so you instantly know which order they're asking about.
</Callout>

## Analytics Integration

Track customer behavior on your storefront from **Online Store > Tracking**:

- **Google Tag Manager**: Add your GTM container ID to track page views, add-to-cart events, checkouts, and purchases
- **Facebook Pixel**: Add your Pixel ID to track conversions and build retargeting audiences
- **TikTok Pixel**: Add your TikTok Pixel ID to measure TikTok ad performance

See [Google Tags](/docs/integrations/google-tags), [Facebook Pixel](/docs/integrations/facebook-pixel), and [TikTok Pixel](/docs/integrations/tiktok-pixel) for detailed setup instructions.
