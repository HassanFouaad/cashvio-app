## What is the Online Store?

The **Online Store** (or storefront) is a customer-facing website where people can browse your products, add items to cart, and place orders online. Each store in Cashvio can have its own storefront accessible via a unique subdomain (e.g., `mystore.cash-vio.com`).

Want a store without monthly fees? See how to [create a free online store](/features/free-online-store) synced with your free POS.

<Callout title="Important">
Cashvio supports **subdomain-based storefronts only** (format: `yourstorename.cash-vio.com`). Custom domains are not currently available.
</Callout>

## How Customers Experience Your Store

When customers visit your storefront, they see:

### Home Page
- **Hero images**: Up to 5 large banner images you configure with display order control
- **Featured categories**: Your product categories for easy browsing
- **Featured products**: A selection of your active products

### Product Browsing
- **All Products** page with search, sorting, and filtering
- **Category pages** showing products in each category
- **Product detail** pages with images, descriptions, variant selection, pricing, and **share buttons** (WhatsApp, Facebook, copy link; see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store))
- **Review stars on product cards**: Products with approved [reviews](/docs/catalogue/product-reviews) show their star rating and review count everywhere they're listed
- **Recently viewed**: A strip of the customer's recently viewed products appears on the home page and under each product page, making it easy to jump back
- **Sorting options**: Sort by newest, name, price low to high, or price high to low
- **Filters**: Filter by category, in-stock only
- **Hide Out of Stock**: Option to hide products that are out of stock (configurable in store settings)

### Shopping Cart
- Customers add products to their cart
- They can adjust quantities, remove items, and see the order summary
- The cart shows subtotal, shipping, tax, and total
- **Stock validation**: The cart checks product availability and maximum order quantities
- **Minimum order nudge**: If you set a [minimum order value](/docs/stores/payment-methods#minimum-order-value), the cart shows a progress bar and "Add X more to place your order" until the minimum is reached

### Checkout
- **Fulfillment selection**: Delivery or Pickup
- **Payment method selection**: Cash or Receipt (with receipt image upload)
- **Delivery zones**: If configured, only your delivery area countries and cities are available
- **Order preview**: Full breakdown of costs before placing the order
- **Minimum order validation**: Enforces minimum order value if configured

See [Online Checkout Flow](/docs/online-store/checkout-flow) for the full checkout process.

### Language Support
- Customers can switch between **English** and **Arabic**
- The entire storefront supports RTL (right-to-left) for Arabic
- Product names, categories, and checkout forms display in the selected language
- **Language-specific links**: Add `?lang=ar` or `?lang=en` to any store link to open it in that language (great for sharing; see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store#share-in-a-specific-language)). Search engines are also told about both language versions of every page automatically

### Theme
- Customers can toggle between **light** and **dark** mode

<Callout title="Note">
The storefront supports **light and dark mode only**. Custom themes, colors, fonts, or layout customization beyond the mode toggle are not available.
</Callout>

### Announcement Bar
- An optional **promo strip at the very top of every page** (above the header) in your brand color, perfect for offers like "Free delivery on orders over 500 EGP"
- Configured per language in **Online Store > Content & SEO > Announcement Bar & Footer Text**; leave it empty to hide the bar

### Footer
- **Copyright notice** with your store name
- Your optional **custom footer text** (a personal message, address, or story) configured in **Content & SEO**
- **"Powered by Cashvio"** attribution with a link to the Cashvio website and a "Create your free online store" link
- Links to your **static pages** (terms, privacy, refund policy, etc.)
- **Social media links** if configured
- On mobile, a **bottom navigation bar** replaces the footer with quick access to Home, Categories, Products, Cart, and Contact. The custom footer text appears in the mobile **Information** sheet

## Configuring Your Online Store

In the portal, open **Online Store** from the sidebar (or **Settings > Stores > your store > Online Store tab**). If the storefront hasn't been created yet, a **Create** button sets it up.

Settings are organized in five sections: **General**, **Appearance**, **Content & SEO**, **Social Media Links**, and **Tracking**. In **General** you can also switch the storefront **Active / Inactive** and toggle **Hide Out of Stock**.

### Store Subdomain Configuration

Each store can have its own unique subdomain:

- **Subdomain Format** — `yourstorename.cash-vio.com`
- **Subdomain Setup** — Configure your store's subdomain in the Online Store settings
- **Access** — Once configured, your storefront is accessible at the subdomain URL
- **Sharing** — Copy the link or **print a QR poster** customers can scan to order — see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store)

<Callout title="Important">
Cashvio supports **subdomain-based storefronts only**. Custom domains are not currently available. Choose your subdomain carefully as it becomes part of your store's web address.
</Callout>

### What You Can Configure

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Store Logo** | Your storefront logo (JPEG/PNG/WEBP, up to 2 MB) |
| **Hero Images** | Up to 5 home-page banners, each with an optional **click-through link** (great for promotions) |
| **Special Products** | Up to 10 featured products pinned on the home page, reorderable by drag and drop |
| **SEO Settings** | Meta title (60 chars), meta description (160 chars), and favicon |
| **Announcement Bar & Footer Text** | Bilingual promo text shown at the top of every page, and a custom footer message |
| **Social Media Links** | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, website URL, and contact phone/email — configured in the **Social Media Links** section |
| **Hide Out of Stock Items** | Hide products with zero inventory from the storefront |
| **Tracking** | Google Tag Manager, Facebook Pixel, and TikTok Pixel IDs |
| **Static Pages** | Refund Policy, Shipping Policy, and About Us — each in English and Arabic with a rich text editor |

## What Affects Your Online Store

Your storefront is connected to the business portal. Changes you make in the portal are reflected on the storefront:

| Portal Setting | Storefront Effect |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Products** | Products with "Active" status appear on the store |
| **Categories** | Active categories appear in navigation and browsing |
| **Prices** | Selling prices from product variants are displayed |
| **Fulfillment Methods** | Storefront-enabled methods (Delivery, Pickup) appear during checkout — configured separately from portal methods |
| **Delivery Zones** | Available delivery areas and fees during checkout |
| **Storefront Payment Methods** | Cash and Receipt appear during checkout based on your configuration |
| **Service Fees** | Delivery and service fees are applied during checkout |

<Callout title="Important">
Only **active** products and categories appear on the storefront. Inactive items are hidden from customers. If "Hide Out of Stock Items" is enabled, products with zero inventory are also hidden.
</Callout>

## Managing Hero Images

Hero images are the large banners at the top of your storefront home page:

1. Open **Online Store > Appearance**.
2. **Upload images** — up to 5 (JPEG/PNG/WEBP, 5 MB each; ~1920×600px looks best). Images display in the order you upload them.
3. **Add a link** (optional) — give any banner a click-through URL, e.g., to a category or promotion page.
4. **Remove images** you no longer want.

<Callout title="Tip">
Use high-quality, visually appealing images that represent your brand. Keep file sizes reasonable for fast loading times.
</Callout>

## Featured (Special) Products

Pin up to **10 products** on your storefront home page:

1. Open **Online Store > Appearance > Special Products**.
2. Click **Add** and pick a product (each product can be added once).
3. **Drag and drop** to set the display order.
4. Remove products from the list any time.

<Callout title="Note">
Only **Active** products display properly — the list warns you if a pinned product is inactive.
</Callout>

## SEO Settings

Improve your store's visibility in search engines:

- **Meta Title** — The title that appears in search engine results and browser tabs (recommended: 50-60 characters)
- **Meta Description** — A brief description of your store (recommended: 150-160 characters)
- **Favicon** — Upload a small icon (16x16 or 32x32px) that appears in browser tabs and bookmarks

<Callout title="Tip">
Write compelling meta titles and descriptions that include relevant keywords. This helps customers find your store in search results.
</Callout>

## Announcement Bar & Footer Text

Promote offers and add a personal touch, from **Online Store > Content & SEO > Announcement Bar & Footer Text**:

1. Enter your **Announcement (English)** and **Announcement (Arabic)** — e.g., "Free delivery on orders over 500 EGP". The bar appears at the very top of every storefront page in your brand color, in the language the customer is browsing in.
2. Enter your **Footer text (English)** and **Footer text (Arabic)** — a short message shown near the bottom of the storefront footer.
3. Click **Save Content**.

- If only one language is filled in, it's shown for both languages.
- Clear a field and save to **hide** the bar or footer message.

<Callout title="Tip">
Announcing a real offer works best — for example, pair "Free delivery on orders over 500 EGP" with the store's [Free Delivery Threshold](/docs/stores/delivery-zones#free-delivery-threshold) so the storefront actually waives the fee automatically.
</Callout>

## Static Pages

Create informative pages for your customers:

### Creating a Static Page

1. Go to **Online Store** settings.
2. Navigate to **Static Pages**.
3. Select the page type (Refund Policy, Shipping Policy, or About Us).
4. Use the **rich text editor** to format your content:
   - Bold and italic text
   - Bulleted and numbered lists
   - Links to other pages or websites
   - Headings and paragraphs
5. Click **Save**.

These pages are automatically linked in your storefront footer and can be accessed by customers at any time.

## Analytics Integration

Track customer behavior on your storefront with:

- **Google Tag Manager** — Add your GTM container ID to track page views, add-to-cart events, checkouts, and purchases
- **Facebook Pixel** — Add your Pixel ID to track conversions and build retargeting audiences
- **TikTok Pixel** — Add your TikTok Pixel ID to measure TikTok ad performance

See [Google Tags](/docs/integrations/google-tags), [Facebook Pixel](/docs/integrations/facebook-pixel), and [TikTok Pixel](/docs/integrations/tiktok-pixel) for detailed setup instructions.
