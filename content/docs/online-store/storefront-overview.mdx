## What is the Online Store?

The **Online Store** (or storefront) is a customer-facing website where people can browse your products, add items to cart, and place orders online. Each store in Cashvio can have its own storefront accessible via a unique subdomain (e.g., `mystore.cash-vio.com`).

Want a store without monthly fees? See how to [create a free online store](/features/free-online-store) synced with your free POS.

<Callout title="Important">
Cashvio supports **subdomain-based storefronts only** (format: `yourstorename.cash-vio.com`). Custom domains are not currently available.
</Callout>

## How Customers Experience Your Store

When customers visit your storefront, they see:

### Home Page
- **Hero images**: Up to 5 large banner images with display order control (see [Storefront Appearance](/docs/online-store/storefront-appearance))
- **Featured categories**: Your product categories for easy browsing
- **Featured products**: A selection of your active products

### Product Browsing
- **All Products** page with search, sorting, and filtering
- **Category pages** showing products in each category
- **Product detail** pages with images, descriptions, variant selection, pricing, and **share buttons** (WhatsApp, Facebook, copy link; see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store))
- **Review stars on product cards**: Products with approved [reviews](/docs/catalogue/product-reviews) show their star rating and review count everywhere they're listed
- **Recently viewed**: A strip of the customer's recently viewed products on the home page and under each product page
- **Related products**: On each product page, more items from the same category ("You may also like")
- **Sorting and filters**: Sort by newest, name, or price; filter by category or in-stock only
- **Hide Out of Stock**: When enabled in **Online Store > General**, out-of-stock products stay out of the catalog, search, category pages, and featured (special) products on the home page. Direct product links also return not found, so shares and search results stay consistent.

### Shopping Cart
- Customers add products, adjust quantities, and see subtotal, shipping, tax, and total
- **Stock validation**: The cart checks product availability and maximum order quantities
- **Minimum order nudge**: If you set a [minimum order value](/docs/stores/storefront-payment-methods), the cart shows a progress bar until the minimum is reached

### Checkout
Customers choose delivery or pickup, select payment (Cash or Receipt), confirm delivery zones and fees, and review the full order breakdown before placing the order.

See [Online Checkout Flow](/docs/online-store/checkout-flow) for the full checkout process.

### Language Support
- Customers can switch between **English** and **Arabic**
- The entire storefront supports RTL (right-to-left) for Arabic
- Add `?lang=ar` or `?lang=en` to any store link to open it in that language (see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store#share-in-a-specific-language))

### Theme
- The look of the whole storefront (colors, fonts, corner style) comes from the [theme you pick](/docs/online-store/storefront-themes)
- Customers can toggle between **light** and **dark** mode, and every theme includes both palettes

### Announcement Bar
An optional **promo strip at the very top of every page** in your theme's primary color. Configure bilingual text in [Storefront SEO & Content](/docs/online-store/storefront-seo-content); leave it empty to hide the bar.

### Footer
Copyright, optional custom footer text, static page links, social icons, and **"Powered by Cashvio"** attribution. On mobile, a bottom navigation bar replaces the footer. Configure footer text in [Storefront SEO & Content](/docs/online-store/storefront-seo-content).

### WhatsApp Chat Button
A **floating WhatsApp button** on every page lets customers start a chat in one tap. Configure the number and toggle in [Social Media & WhatsApp](/docs/online-store/social-whatsapp-button).

## Portal Settings

In the portal, open **Online Store** from the sidebar (or **Settings > Stores > your store > Online Store tab**). If the storefront hasn't been created yet, a **Create** button sets it up.

From **General**, switch the storefront **Active / Inactive** and toggle **Hide Out of Stock**. Settings are grouped into **General**, **Theme**, **Featured Products**, **Pages**, **SEO**, **Contacts & Social**, and **Tracking**. Logo, hero banners, and announcement text live inside the Theme Builder, next to the live preview. SEO and contacts each have their own tab. Use the cards below for step-by-step guides on each area.

### Tracking IDs

Under **Online Store > Tracking**, add Google Tag Manager, Facebook Pixel, and TikTok Pixel IDs to measure storefront behavior. See [Google Tags](/docs/integrations/google-tags), [Facebook Pixel](/docs/integrations/facebook-pixel), and [TikTok Pixel](/docs/integrations/tiktok-pixel) for setup details.

## What Affects Your Online Store

Your storefront is connected to the business portal. Changes you make in the portal are reflected on the storefront:

| Portal Setting | Storefront Effect |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Products** | Products appear when their status is "Active" and [Available on Online Store](/docs/online-store/product-availability) is on |
| **Categories** | Active categories appear in navigation and browsing |
| **Prices** | Selling prices from product variants are displayed |
| **Fulfillment Methods** | Storefront-enabled methods (Delivery, Pickup) appear during checkout, configured separately from portal methods |
| **Delivery Zones** | Available delivery areas and fees during checkout |
| **Storefront Payment Methods** | Cash and Receipt appear during checkout based on your configuration |
| **Service Fees** | Delivery and service fees are applied during checkout |

<Callout title="Important">
Only **active** products and categories appear on the storefront. Inactive items are hidden from customers. Active products also need [Available on Online Store](/docs/online-store/product-availability) turned on, which lets you sell something at the counter without listing it here. If "Hide Out of Stock Items" is enabled, products with zero inventory are also hidden.
</Callout>
