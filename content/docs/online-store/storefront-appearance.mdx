Use **Online Store > Appearance** (and **General** for logo and subdomain) to shape the visual identity customers see when they land on your storefront.

## General and Appearance Tabs

- **General**: Store subdomain, **Active / Inactive** toggle, **Hide Out of Stock**, store logo, and your **Online Store URL** (copy link or print QR poster; see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store))
- **Appearance**: Hero banner images and **Special Products** pinned to the home page

## Store Logo

Upload your storefront logo from **Online Store > General**:

- **Formats**: JPEG, PNG, or WEBP
- **Max size**: 2 MB
- The logo appears in the storefront header and checkout

<Callout title="Tip">
Use a clear logo on a transparent or solid background. Square or horizontal layouts work best in the header.
</Callout>

## Managing Hero Images

Hero images are the large banners at the top of your storefront home page:

1. Open **Online Store > Appearance**.
2. **Upload images**: up to 5 (JPEG/PNG/WEBP, 5 MB each; ~1920×600px looks best). Images display in the order you upload them.
3. **Add a link** (optional): give any banner a click-through URL, e.g., to a category or promotion page.
4. **Remove images** you no longer want.

<Callout title="Tip">
Use high-quality, visually appealing images that represent your brand. Keep file sizes reasonable for fast loading times.
</Callout>

## Featured (Special) Products

Pin up to **10 products** on your storefront home page:

1. Open **Online Store > Appearance > Special Products**.
2. Click **Add** and pick a product (each product can be added once).
3. **Drag and drop** to set the display order.
4. Remove products from the list any time.

<Callout title="Note">
Only **Active** products display properly; the list warns you if a pinned product is inactive. If **Hide Out of Stock** is on (**Online Store > General**), out-of-stock special products are also hidden from the home page, same as the catalog.
</Callout>

## Theme (Light and Dark Mode)

Customers can switch between **light** and **dark** mode from the storefront header. The toggle applies to the entire site, including product pages and checkout.

<Callout title="Note">
The storefront supports **light and dark mode only**. Custom themes, colors, fonts, or layout customization beyond the mode toggle are not available.
</Callout>
