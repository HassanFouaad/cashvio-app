Your storefront's visual identity is shaped in two places: the **Theme Builder** (logo, banners, colors, and design, all beside a live preview) and the Online Store settings (featured products, subdomain, and status).

## Where Everything Lives

- **General**: Store subdomain, **Active / Inactive** toggle, **Hide Out of Stock**, and your **Online Store URL** (copy link or print QR poster; see [Sharing Your Store](/docs/online-store/sharing-your-store))
- **Featured Products**: products pinned to the home page
- **Theme Builder**: logo, hero banners, design, colors, fonts, content, SEO, and social links

## Store Logo

Upload your storefront logo from **Online Store > Theme > Open Theme Builder > Logo**, right beside the live preview:

- **Formats**: JPEG, PNG, or WEBP
- **Max size**: 2 MB
- The logo appears in the storefront header and checkout

<Callout title="Tip">
Use a clear logo on a transparent or solid background. Square or horizontal layouts work best in the header.
</Callout>

## Managing Hero Images

Hero images are the large banners at the top of your storefront home page:

1. Open **Online Store > Theme > Open Theme Builder > Banners**.
2. **Upload images**: up to 5 (JPEG/PNG/WEBP, 5 MB each; ~1920×600px looks best). Drag to re-order; the live preview updates after every change.
3. **Add a link** (optional): give any banner a click-through URL, e.g., to a category or promotion page.
4. **Remove images** you no longer want.

Every theme shows ALL your banners: the Classic carousel slides through them, and the split and full-width hero designs rotate through them gently with dot indicators.

<Callout title="Tip">
Use high-quality, visually appealing images that represent your brand. Keep file sizes reasonable for fast loading times.
</Callout>

## Featured (Special) Products

Pin up to **10 products** on your storefront home page:

1. Open **Online Store > Featured Products**.
2. Click **Add** and pick a product (each product can be added once).
3. **Drag and drop** to set the display order.
4. Remove products from the list any time.

<Callout title="Note">
A product must be [available on your online store](/docs/online-store/product-availability) before you can pin it; hidden products appear dimmed and tagged in the picker. Only **Active** products display properly, and the list warns you if a pinned product is inactive or no longer published. If **Hide Out of Stock** is on (**Online Store > General**), out-of-stock special products are also hidden from the home page, same as the catalog.
</Callout>

## Theme (Light and Dark Mode)

Customers can switch between **light** and **dark** mode from the storefront header. The toggle applies to the entire site, including product pages and checkout.

<Callout title="Tip">
Your store's colors, fonts, and corner style come from its **theme**. Pick one of the designed themes and customize it in [Storefront Themes](/docs/online-store/storefront-themes); every theme includes a matching light and dark palette.
</Callout>
