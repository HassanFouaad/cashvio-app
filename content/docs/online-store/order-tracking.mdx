## Overview

Every Cashvio storefront ships with a **Track Order** page at `/track` (for example `yourstore.cash-vio.com/track`). Customers look up their order with two things:

- The **order number** they received on the order-success page
- The **phone number** they entered at checkout

No customer account or login is required.

<Callout title="Where customers find it">
The order-success page shows the order number with a copy button and a **Track order** link. The tracking page is also linked from the storefront footer.
</Callout>

## What Customers See

The tracking page shows a live view of the order:

- **Status timeline** — the order's progress through fulfillment
- **Order items** — products and quantities
- **Totals** — subtotal, discounts, fees, and the final amount

The timeline follows the order's **fulfillment status**:

| Status | What it means for the customer |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **Pending** | Order received, not started yet |
| **Preparing** | The store is working on the order |
| **Ready** | Ready for pickup (pickup orders) |
| **Delivering** | Out for delivery (delivery orders) |
| **Completed** | Order fulfilled |
| **Cancelled** | Order was cancelled |

## Keeping the Page Accurate

The tracking page reflects whatever fulfillment status the order has **right now**. To keep customers informed:

1. Open the order from **Sales > All Sales**.
2. Update the **fulfillment status** as the order progresses (Preparing → Ready → Delivering → Completed).
3. Optionally click **WhatsApp customer** to send a status message — the tracking link gives customers a place to check anytime without messaging you.

<Callout title="Fewer 'where is my order?' calls">
Merchants who update statuses promptly get fewer WhatsApp check-ins — the customer's answer is always one page away.
</Callout>

## Requirements & Tips

- The phone number must **match the one entered at checkout** — customers who typo their phone at checkout won't be able to look the order up; you can always share the status manually from order details.
- Tracking is available for **online store orders**. POS sales are shared via the digital receipt instead.
- The page follows your storefront language — customers can switch between Arabic and English.

## FAQs

**Q: Do customers need an account to track orders?**
A: No. The order number plus the checkout phone number is all they need.

**Q: Can a customer see someone else's order?**
A: No. Both the order number and the matching phone number are required, and lookups are rate-limited.

**Q: What shows for pickup orders vs delivery orders?**
A: The timeline adapts — pickup orders show **Ready** for collection, delivery orders show **Delivering** on the way.
