## What is a Supplier?

A **supplier** (also called a vendor) is a company or person you buy products from to stock your stores. Keeping supplier records in Cashvio lets you create [purchase orders](/docs/suppliers/purchase-orders) against them and see the full ordering history per supplier.

## Adding a Supplier

1. Go to **Inventory > Suppliers** (a tab in the Inventory section), or use the supplier picker when creating a purchase order.
2. Click **Add Supplier**.
3. Fill in the details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Supplier Name** | The supplier's business name (required) |
| **Email** | Contact email (optional) |
| **Phone** | Contact phone (optional) |
| **Address** | Supplier's address (optional) |

4. Click **Save**.

## Viewing Your Suppliers

The **Suppliers** page shows a list of all suppliers with their name, email, and phone. Search by **name, email, or phone** to find a supplier.

## Supplier Details

Click on a supplier to see:

- **Supplier information**: name and contact details
- **Purchase orders**: the full history of purchase orders with this supplier, with the same actions as the main PO list (view, approve, cancel, receive)
- **Create Purchase Order**: starts a new PO with this supplier pre-selected

## Editing a Supplier

1. Open the supplier details (or use the row menu on the list).
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Update the name or contact information.
4. Click **Save**.

## Deleting a Supplier

1. Open the supplier details.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

<Callout title="Tip">
Set up your suppliers before creating purchase orders, so you can pick them quickly when restocking.
</Callout>
