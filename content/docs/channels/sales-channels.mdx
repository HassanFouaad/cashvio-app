## What are Sales Channels?

A **sales channel** records where an order came from, so your reports can show which sources bring you the most business.

## Predefined Channels

Cashvio ships with predefined channels, each with its own icon:

- **Web** (your online storefront)
- **Portal** (orders your team creates)
- **Facebook**
- **Instagram**
- **WhatsApp**
- **Telegram**
- **TikTok**

Predefined channels cannot be deleted.

## Creating a Custom Channel

For any other source (e.g., "Phone Orders", a marketplace, a delivery app):

1. Go to **Settings > Channels**.
2. Click **Create Channel**.
3. Enter the **channel name** (up to 50 characters) and save.

You can create up to **20 custom channels**. Custom channels can be **deleted**; renaming is not currently supported, so delete and re-create instead.

## Enabling Channels per Store

Which channels are usable is controlled **per store**:

1. Go to **Settings > Stores**, open the store, and select the **Channels** tab.
2. Toggle each channel **active or inactive** for that store.

## Using Channels on Orders

When your team creates a sale in the portal or POS, pick the channel that matches where the sale came from (Portal, WhatsApp, a custom channel, and so on). You can filter the [orders list](/docs/orders/managing-orders) by channel.

### Online storefront orders

Orders placed on your online storefront are tagged with the **Web** channel automatically. You do not need to choose a channel for those orders. This is separate from **Order Source**, which still shows **Storefront** for online orders and **Web** for orders created in the portal.

## Viewing Channel Performance

Open **[Reports > Overview](/docs/reports/analytics-reports)** to see the **Sales by Channel** chart, showing which channels bring the most revenue and orders. Online storefront sales appear under **Web**. Orders with no channel selected (for example older portal sales) are not included in the chart slices; the report can still show how many orders are missing a channel.

<Callout title="Tip">
Set up channels that match your real sales sources. Accurate channel attribution tells you exactly where to invest your marketing effort.
</Callout>
