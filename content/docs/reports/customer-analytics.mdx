---
title: Customer Analytics Report
description: "Track acquisition, retention, lifetime value, and spending patterns on the Customers tab in Reports."
---

The **Customers** report shows customer behavior patterns so you can grow repeat business and spot high-value buyers.

## What you see

- **Customer Acquisition**: How many new customers over time
- **Repeat Customer Rate**: Percentage of returning customers
- **Customer Lifetime Value**: Average total spending per customer
- **Customer Segmentation**: Grouping customers by spending patterns
