The **Staff** report shows the completed sales each team member rang up in the period, so you can see how the work is shared and where discounts and returns happen.

## Headline and key figures

The headline gives team sales, how many staff sold, and the average per person, for example "EGP 42300.00 team sales across 4 staff members; EGP 10575.00 per person."

The tiles are **Team sales**, **Active staff**, **Average per staff** and **Team average order**.

## Sales by staff

**Sales by staff** lists every staff member who rang up a completed sale, highest sales first, with:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Staff member** | Name and email |
| **Sales** | Completed sales they rang up |
| **Orders** | Number of those orders |
| **Average order** | Sales divided by orders |
| **Items per order** | Average items in each order |
| **Discount rate** | Discounts as a share of their sales before discounts |
| **Returns processed** | Returns they handled |
| **Share of sales** | Their part of the team's sales |

Select a row to open the user's details when your role can view users. The list is a plain ranking: there are no medals or labels for individual staff.

## Discounts and returns by staff

- **Discount rate by staff** shows the staff members who gave discounts, highest rate first.
- **Returns processed by staff** shows how many returns each staff member handled.

Choose **Export CSV** beside the headline to download the **Sales by staff** table.
