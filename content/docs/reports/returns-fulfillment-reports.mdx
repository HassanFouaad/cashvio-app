Use the **Returns** and **Fulfillment** tabs to understand why products come back and how completed sales reach customers.

## Returns Analytics

The **Returns** report helps you understand return patterns:

- **Return Rate**: Percentage of orders with returns
- **Return Reasons**: Breakdown of why products are returned
- **Returns by Product**: Which products are returned most
- **Return Value**: Total monetary value of returns

## Fulfillment Analytics

The **Fulfillment** report shows how completed sales reach your customers:

- **Fulfillment Rate**: Share of sales already handed over or delivered, plus how many are still pending
- **Orders by Method**: How sales split between in-store, pickup, delivery, and dine-in, with revenue, average order value, and fees collected per method
- **Fulfillment Status**: Where sales currently stand (pending, preparing, ready, delivering, completed)
- **Delivery Performance**: Delivery order count and revenue, the delivery fees you earned, and the average fee per order (shown once you have delivery orders)
- **Fulfillment by Store**: The pickup / delivery / dine-in mix and the pending count per store

<Callout title="Note">
Use the Fulfillment tab to decide where to invest: if delivery brings a high average order value but few orders, promoting it may pay off, and a rising pending count at one store is an early staffing signal.
</Callout>
