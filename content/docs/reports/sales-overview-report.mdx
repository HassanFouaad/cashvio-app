The **Overview** tab shows your overall business performance at a glance. Use it to spot trends, compare stores and channels, and export data for spreadsheets.

## Key metrics

- **Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Total Orders, Avg Order Value**, plus gross margin, items sold, discounts, tax, and new customers. Changes appear only for metrics with comparison data

## Charts available

- **Sales Trends**: choose **Revenue** or **Orders** to inspect one measure at a time, each on its own scale
- **Sales by Hour / Day**: Hourly breakdown in day view, daily in week/month view
- **Sales by Store**: Revenue share per store. Select a store name or slice to open its orders for the same reporting period when your role has access.
- **Sales by Channel**: Which channels generate the most revenue (online storefront sales count under **Web**; orders without a channel are excluded from the slices)
- **Sales by Payment Method**: How customers pay
- **Top Products**: A ranked table of product variants, SKUs, quantities, revenue, and average selling prices. On a phone, each row becomes a readable card.
- **Online Store Visits**: Storefront visitors, split into unique, new, and returning

## CSV export

Choose **Export CSV** beside **Sales Overview** to download the same series shown in **Sales Trends**. Day exports include the hour; week and month exports include the date. Periods with no sales are included with zero values.

## Reading the overview

The headline keeps revenue, gross profit, orders, and average order value visible. Open **Explore financial and customer details** for the remaining metrics. Use the section links to jump to **Performance**, **Top Products**, **Where sales come from**, or **Online Store Visits**.

The reporting period and timezone appear above the overview. Use **Select Date** to open the calendar, including the year selector for older reports. **Custom** lets you set **From** and **To** dates. The start must not be after the end, and reports do not include future dates.

Under **Sales Trends**, open **View sales trend data** to read exact dates, revenue, order counts, and average order values in a table or mobile cards. This view matches the chart, including periods with no sales. Use **View Sales** on a row to open that day's orders when your role has access. Both disclosures and the date selector work with the keyboard.

Each sales-source chart includes readable labels and exact values, so you do not need to hover over a slice. Store labels are keyboard-accessible actions. **Product details** opens a product from **Top Products** when your role allows it.

If a section cannot load, it shows its own error message and **Retry** when available. Other sections remain usable. If a refresh fails temporarily, earlier figures stay visible with a warning that they may be out of date. CSV export is unavailable while trend data is loading or has an error.
