The **Overview** tab shows your overall business performance at a glance. Use it to spot trends, compare stores and channels, and export data for spreadsheets.

## Key metrics

- **Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value**, plus gross margin, items sold, discounts, tax, and new customers, each with a change vs. the prior period

## Charts available

- **Sales Trends**: Sales over time, with peak and lowest points
- **Sales by Hour / Day**: Hourly breakdown in day view, daily in week/month view
- **Sales by Store**: Revenue share per store (click a slice to open that store's orders)
- **Sales by Channel**: Which channels generate the most revenue (online storefront sales count under **Web**; orders without a channel are excluded from the slices)
- **Sales by Payment Method**: How customers pay
- **Top Products**: Best-selling products by quantity and revenue
- **Store Visits**: Storefront visitors, split into unique, new, and returning

## CSV export

The Overview tab supports **CSV export** of the sales data for use in spreadsheets.
