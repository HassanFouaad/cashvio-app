---

The **Overview** report answers how your sales are going in one screen. It uses the period, **Compare with** and **Store** choices from the bar above the report.

## Headline and key figures

The headline gives total sales, the change against the comparison dates and the number of orders, for example "EGP 42300.00 in sales, up 12% vs your comparison dates. 318 orders." With **No comparison** the change is left out.

Four tiles follow: **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Avg Order Value** and **Gross Profit**. Each change names what it is compared with. Select **Gross Profit** to open the **Profit** report for the same period.

## Sales trend

**Sales trend** charts **Revenue** or **Orders** by hour, day, week or month, depending on how long the period is. With a comparison, a dashed line shows the previous period or the same period last year, and a dot marks the best bucket. Select a point to open its completed orders in **Sales**: an hour or a day opens that whole day, and a week or a month opens its days within the period. Choose **Show as table** to read the same figures as a table, with **View orders** on each row.

## Needs attention and top products

- **Needs attention** lists up to three critical issues or warnings for the period, or says that nothing needs your attention. **Open insights** opens the **Insights** report.
- **Top products** ranks the five best-selling variants with quantity, revenue and average price. Select a row to open the product.

## Where sales come from

Switch between **Stores**, **Payment methods** and **Channels** to see revenue by source, largest first, with each source's share and order count. Select a row to open the matching completed orders.

## More metrics and visitors

**More metrics** shows the rest of the period's figures: items sold, discounts, tax, fees, customers, peak hour and best day. When your plan includes the online store and your role can view stores, **Online store visitors** shows visits, unique, new and returning visitors, and visitors by store.

## CSV export

Choose **Export CSV** beside the headline to download the trend rows: date, revenue, orders and average order value, plus the comparison revenue when you compare.

<Callout title="Tip">
  Use the arrows beside the period to step back one period at a time, and
  **Compare with** **Same period last year** to check seasonal months.
</Callout>
