title: Business Insights Report
description: "The business health score, area scores, and insights by severity with recommended actions on the Insights report."
---

The **Insights** report checks your numbers for the selected period against simple rules and turns what it finds into plain-language guidance.

## Headline and health score

The headline sums up the period, for example "Business health 72 of 100; 1 needs attention, 2 warnings, 3 wins." **Updated** under it shows how long ago the insights were calculated.

**Business health** is a score out of 100 with a progress bar. Next to it, four area tiles score revenue, profitability, customer health and operations. Each area shows:

- its score out of 100,
- the change in the figure behind it against the previous period: revenue for revenue, gross margin for profitability, new customers for customer health, and the return rate for operations (a falling return rate counts as an improvement),
- a short list of what drives the score, such as healthy margins or low retention.

## Insight sections

Insights appear in up to four sections, and empty sections are hidden:

| Section | What it holds |
|---------|---------------|
| **Needs attention** | Critical issues to act on now |
| **Warnings** | Problems building up, such as an elevated return rate or low retention |
| **Opportunities** | Openings to grow, such as your peak hours |
| **Wins** | What improved, such as revenue growth or a better margin |

Use **Category** beside the headline to show only one area, such as **Profit** or **Returns**, in every section. Choose **All categories** to see everything again.

## Reading an insight

Each card shows a title, a short explanation, and the change when there is one. A line such as "Shown because return rate is at or above 5%." tells you which rule the insight comes from. Up to two recommended actions follow.

Use **Open report** to open the report that explains the insight for the same period, and **Open record** to open the product, customer, store or staff member behind it when your role allows it. Products open the **Products** list filtered by the product name.

When there are no insights, the report says so. Choose a longer period to give the rules more data.

<Callout title="Note">
  Insights are generated from your actual sales, product, customer, and returns
  data over the selected period, and always compare with the previous period of
  the same length.
</Callout>
