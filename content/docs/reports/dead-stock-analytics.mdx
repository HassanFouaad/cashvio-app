## Open the report in Cashvio

1. Go to **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Open the **Dead stock** tab.
3. Pick a window: **30d**, **60d**, or **90d**.

You need analytics on your plan and permission to view orders (same as the other report tabs).

## The headline

At the top you see a line like:

> **EGP 12,000 sleeping on your shelves**

That is the **total value at cost** of active products that still have units on hand and had **no completed sales** in the selected window (or were never sold). Below it: how many SKUs and units that covers.

## The product list

Each row shows:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Product** | Name, variant, and SKU |
| **Store** | Which store holds the stock |
| **Units** | Quantity on hand |
| **Value at cost** | Units × cost per unit |
| **Last sale** | Date of the last completed sale, or **Never sold** |

Tap a row to open that product in the catalogue.

## How stock is counted as "dead"

A SKU×store line appears when:

- Quantity on hand is greater than zero
- The product is **active**
- There is no **completed** sale for that variant at that store in the last 30, 60, or 90 days (depending on the toggle), or there has never been a sale

Cost comes from the inventory cost on hand, not the selling price.

<Callout title="Tip">
Start with **90d** to find the deepest sleepers, then tighten to **30d** when you want a weekly cleanup list for discounts or purchase pauses.
</Callout>

## FAQs

**Q: Why is something listed if I sold it yesterday?**
A: Only **completed** sales count. Pending or cancelled orders do not clear dead stock.

**Q: Can I export the list?**
A: Not from this tab yet. Use the headline and table for decisions, or open products from the rows.
