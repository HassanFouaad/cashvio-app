## Open the report in Cashvio

1. Go to **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Open **Dead stock** in the **Inventory** group.
3. Pick a window beside the headline: **30 days**, **60 days**, or **90 days**.
4. If you can see more than one store, use **Store** above the report to check one store.

You need analytics on your plan and the **View Inventory** permission. The report shows your stock as it is now, so it has no period or comparison.

## The headline and key figures

At the top you see a sentence like:

> **EGP 12000.00 tied up in 12 products with no sale for 30+ days (340 units).**

That is the **total value at cost** of active products that still have units on hand and had **no completed sales** in the selected window (or were never sold).

Three tiles follow: **Sleeping value**, **Products** and **Units**.

## The product list

**Products** lists the stock lines by sleeping value, highest first:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Product** | Name, variant, and SKU |
| **Store** | Which store holds the stock (shown when the list covers more than one store) |
| **Units** | Quantity on hand |
| **Value at cost** | Units × cost per unit |
| **Last sale** | Date of the last completed sale, or **Never sold** |

When there are more sleeping products than the list shows, a line under the title says how many of them you are seeing. Select a row to open that product in the catalogue.

## How stock is counted as "dead"

A SKU×store line appears when:

- Quantity on hand is greater than zero
- The product is **active**
- There is no **completed** sale for that variant at that store in the last 30, 60, or 90 days (depending on the window), or there has never been a sale

Cost comes from the inventory cost on hand, not the selling price.

<Callout title="Tip">
Start with **90 days** to find the deepest sleepers, then tighten to **30 days** when you want a weekly cleanup list for discounts or purchase pauses.
</Callout>

## FAQs

**Q: Why is something listed if I sold it yesterday?**
A: Only **completed** sales count. Pending or cancelled orders do not clear dead stock.

**Q: Can I export the list?**
A: Yes. Choose **Export CSV** beside the headline to download the products with variant, SKU, store, units, unit cost, value at cost and last sale date.
