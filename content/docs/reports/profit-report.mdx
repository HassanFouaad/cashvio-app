---
title: Profit Analysis Report
description: "Read gross margin, cost coverage, the revenue breakdown and product profitability on the Profit report."
---

The **Profit** report shows how much of your sales you keep after the cost of goods. Accurate **cost prices** on each variant are essential for trustworthy numbers.

## Headline and key figures

The headline gives your gross margin on the period's sales and how it moved in percentage points against the previous period, for example "Gross margin 32.5% on EGP 42300.00 in sales, up 3.7 pts vs the previous period."

When some completed orders have no cost price, a note under the headline says what share of orders is affected and that profit is overstated. The same note sits on the **Gross profit** tile.

The four tiles are **Gross profit** (with its change against the previous period), **Gross margin** (change in points), **Net profit** and **Cost of goods sold**.

## Revenue breakdown

**Revenue breakdown** splits your sales before discounts into **Gross profit**, **Cost of goods sold** and **Total Discounts**, each with its share.

## Product profitability

- **Most profitable products**: highest gross profit first.
- **Low-margin products**: lowest gross margin first.

Each row shows the product with its variant and SKU, quantity, revenue, gross profit and margin. The margin badge is green from 30%, amber from 20% to under 30%, and red below 20%. Select a row to open the **Products** list filtered by that product's name.

## Profit by store

When you sell in more than one store, **Profit by store** shows revenue, gross profit, margin, orders and profit per order for each store. Select a store to open its completed orders for the period.

## More metrics and export

**More metrics** shows **Total Discounts**, **Discount rate**, **Net margin** and **Orders without cost** (the number of completed orders without a cost price, with their share).

Choose **Export CSV** beside the headline to download both product lists with quantity, revenue, cost, gross profit and margin.

<Callout title="Note">
  Profit calculations use the **cost price** you set on each product variant.
  Keep cost prices up to date; until then, orders without a cost count their
  full revenue as profit.
</Callout>

## How refunds affect profit

Revenue and product profit are shown after completed refunds. For a partial refund, both revenue and cost are reduced proportionally so the margin reflects the amount you kept. A fully refunded sale contributes no revenue, cost, or profit and is excluded from the most-profitable and low-margin product lists.

<Callout title="Fully refunded periods">
  If every sale in the selected period was fully refunded, revenue and gross
  profit are zero, so the headline says there were no completed sales.
</Callout>
