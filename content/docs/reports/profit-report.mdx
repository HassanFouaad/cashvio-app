---

The **Profit** report helps you understand your margins and where money is made or lost. Accurate **cost prices** on each variant are essential for trustworthy numbers.

## What you see

- **Revenue vs. Cost**: Compare selling prices to cost prices
- **Refunds**: See completed refunds separately from the revenue you kept
- **Profit Margins**: See your gross profit by product or category
- **Cost Analysis**: Track costs by supplier
- **Product Profitability**: Identify your most and least profitable products

<Callout title="Note">
  Profit calculations use the **cost price** you set on each product variant.
  Make sure to keep cost prices up to date for accurate profit reporting.
</Callout>

## How refunds affect profit

Revenue and product profit are shown after completed refunds. For a partial refund, both revenue and cost are reduced proportionally so the margin reflects the amount you kept. A fully refunded sale contributes no revenue, cost, or profit and is excluded from the most-profitable and low-margin product lists.

<Callout title="Fully refunded periods">
  If every sale in the selected period was fully refunded, revenue and gross
  profit are zero, so the gross margin is shown as 0%. Check **Refunds** to
  distinguish this from a period with no sales.
</Callout>
