Go to **Reports** in the sidebar to see how your business is performing. Each tab focuses on a different area; use the cards below for full walkthroughs.

## Accessing Reports

Reports are organized in tabs: **Overview, Profit, Customers, Returns, Fulfillment, Staff, Dead stock, and Insights**.

### Filters (all tabs)

- **View Period**: Day, Week, Month, or a **Custom** date range
- Numbers compare against the **previous period** automatically

## Report tabs at a glance

- **Overview**: Total revenue, orders, and sales trends across stores, channels, and products.
- **Profit**: Margins and product profitability using your cost prices.
- **Customers**: Acquisition, retention, and spending patterns.
- **Returns**: Return rates, reasons, and value by product.
- **Fulfillment**: Delivery, pickup, and dine-in mix plus pending orders.
- **Staff**: Revenue and orders handled per team member.
- **Dead stock**: Inventory that has not sold in 30, 60, or 90 days.
- **Insights**: Health scores and plain-language recommendations from your data.
