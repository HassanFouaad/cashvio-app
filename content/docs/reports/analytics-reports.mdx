## Accessing Reports

Go to **Reports** in the sidebar. Reports are organized in seven tabs: **Overview, Profit, Customers, Returns, Fulfillment, Staff, and Insights**.

### Filters (all tabs)

- **View Period**: Day, Week, Month, or a **Custom** date range
- Numbers compare against the **previous period** automatically

## Sales Overview

The **Overview** tab shows your overall business performance:

- **Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value**, plus gross margin, items sold, discounts, tax, and new customers, each with a change vs. the prior period

### Charts Available
- **Sales Trends**: Sales over time, with peak and lowest points
- **Sales by Hour / Day**: Hourly breakdown in day view, daily in week/month view
- **Sales by Store**: Revenue share per store (click a slice to open that store's orders)
- **Sales by Channel**: Which channels generate the most revenue
- **Sales by Payment Method**: How customers pay
- **Top Products**: Best-selling products by quantity and revenue
- **Store Visits**: Storefront visitors, split into unique, new, and returning

The Overview tab supports **CSV export** of the sales data for use in spreadsheets.

## Profit Analysis

The **Profit** report helps you understand your margins:

- **Revenue vs. Cost**: Compare selling prices to cost prices
- **Profit Margins**: See your gross profit by product or category
- **Cost Analysis**: Track costs by supplier
- **Product Profitability**: Identify your most and least profitable products

<Callout title="Note">
Profit calculations use the **cost price** you set on each product variant. Make sure to keep cost prices up to date for accurate profit reporting.
</Callout>

## Customer Analytics

The **Customers** report shows customer behavior patterns:

- **Customer Acquisition**: How many new customers over time
- **Repeat Customer Rate**: Percentage of returning customers
- **Customer Lifetime Value**: Average total spending per customer
- **Customer Segmentation**: Grouping customers by spending patterns

## Staff Performance

The **Staff** report tracks team member metrics:

- **Sales by Staff**: Revenue generated per team member
- **Orders Processed**: Number of orders handled by each person
- **Performance Trends**: How individual performance changes over time

## Returns Analytics

The **Returns** report helps you understand return patterns:

- **Return Rate**: Percentage of orders with returns
- **Return Reasons**: Breakdown of why products are returned
- **Returns by Product**: Which products are returned most
- **Return Value**: Total monetary value of returns

## Fulfillment Analytics

The **Fulfillment** report shows how completed sales reach your customers:

- **Fulfillment Rate**: Share of sales already handed over or delivered, plus how many are still pending
- **Orders by Method**: How sales split between in-store, pickup, delivery, and dine-in, with revenue, average order value, and fees collected per method
- **Fulfillment Status**: Where sales currently stand (pending, preparing, ready, delivering, completed)
- **Delivery Performance**: Delivery order count and revenue, the delivery fees you earned, and the average fee per order (shown once you have delivery orders)
- **Fulfillment by Store**: The pickup / delivery / dine-in mix and the pending count per store

<Callout title="Note">
Use this tab to decide where to invest: if delivery brings a high average order value but few orders, promoting it may pay off, and a rising pending count at one store is an early staffing signal.
</Callout>

## Business Insights

The **Insights** tab analyzes your numbers and turns them into plain-language guidance:

- **Health scores**: An overall business health score plus per-area scores (revenue, products, customers, profit, stores, returns)
- **Requires Attention**: Critical issues to act on now
- **Warnings and Opportunities**: Emerging risks and growth openings (e.g., peak-hours opportunities, best-seller momentum, high discount alerts)
- **Recent Wins**: What improved recently
- **Recommended actions**: Each insight comes with concrete recommendations, expected impact, and priority

<Callout title="Note">
Insights are generated from your actual sales, product, customer, and returns data over the selected period.
</Callout>
