## Managing Users

Go to **Team** in the sidebar to see all team members in your Cashvio workspace.

The users list shows each user's **name**, **username**, **email**, **status** (Active / Inactive), and **last login**. You can filter by name, username, email, and status.

## Adding a Team Member

1. Go to **Team** and click **Invite User**.
2. Fill in the details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **First Name / Last Name** | The team member's name |
| **Email** | Their email address |
| **Username** | The username they can log in with (login accepts username or email) |
| **Hourly Rate** | Optional; used for staff cost reporting |
| **Select Role** | Choose one role for the user |
| **Business-wide access (all stores)** | Apply the role across the business, including future stores |
| **Assigned stores** | With business-wide access turned off, choose one or more stores for the same role |

3. Click **Save**.

Each user has one role in your business. For example, select **Cashier** and assign Store A and Store B. A Business Admin already has POS access and needs no additional Cashier role.

### Sharing the temporary password

When the user is created, Cashvio generates a **temporary password** and shows it **once** in a dialog with a copy button. Cashvio also emails the invitation, with the username and temporary password, to the team member's email.

<Callout title="Accepting the invitation">
The invitation email has an **Accept invitation** button. Clicking it confirms the team member's email address. Until it is confirmed, Cashvio sends them no other emails, and the portal asks them to confirm it each time they sign in. If the email doesn't arrive, **copy the temporary password and share it with the team member yourself** (in person or through a channel you trust); they can then send themselves a new confirmation link from the portal. If the password is lost, you can set a new one from the user's details page at any time.
</Callout>

## User Details

Click on a user to see their profile:

- **Personal Information**: name, username, email
- **Account Status**: Active / Inactive
- **Role and Store Access**: their role and the stores they can access
- **Last Activity**: most recent sign-in time

### Actions available

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Edit** | Update first name, last name, and hourly rate |
| **Change Password** | Set a new password for the user (useful when the temporary password is lost) |
| **Change User Role** | Choose a replacement role and update business-wide access or assigned stores. The previous role is replaced when you save. |
| **Activate / Deactivate** | Toggle whether the user can log in |

If someone else changes access while you are editing, saving shows a conflict and reloads the current role and stores. Review them before making your changes again. Closing a form with unsaved changes asks whether to discard them.

After a role change, the team member must sign in again to use their new access.

<Callout title="Protected team members">
You can change another team member's **password** or **Active / Inactive** status only when you hold every permission they have, in every store where they have it. For example, a manager of one store can't reset the password of someone with business-wide access. Ask someone with broader access, such as the business owner, to make the change.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Note">
A user's **email cannot be edited** here; email changes go through the verified email-change flow that the user starts from their own [Account Settings](/docs/settings/account-settings).
</Callout>

## Store selection in the portal

Operational pages (orders, inventory, POS, online store settings) use the **selected store** in the URL or picker. Permissions apply in that store context. Switch stores from the page filter or online store picker when you hold access to more than one location.

## Deactivating vs Deleting

- **Deactivate** a user to block their login without losing their history, which is best for staff who leave temporarily.
- Deactivated users can be **re-activated** at any time.

<Callout title="Tip">
Records created by a user (orders, adjustments, returns) always keep their name, so deactivating staff does not affect your reports or audit history.
</Callout>
