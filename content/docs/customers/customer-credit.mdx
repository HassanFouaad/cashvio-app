---
title: Customer Credit (Pay Later)
description: "Sell on credit at the counter, track what each customer owes, and collect the remainder later."
---

**Customer credit** lets trusted customers take goods now and pay later. Each on-credit sale is tied to a **customer profile** so you always know who owes what.

This is separate from [Store Credit](/docs/customers/store-credit): store credit is a prepaid balance (often from refunds). **Amount owed** is unpaid money from on-credit orders.

## Turn It On

1. Go to **Settings > Plan**.
2. Find **Customer Credit (Pay Later)** and switch it on.

The module is off by default on existing accounts. When it is off, all sales must be paid at checkout (except the existing unpaid-order flow for pickup and delivery without credit tracking).

<Callout title="Online store">
On-credit sales work in the **portal and POS only**. Orders placed on your public online store must be paid at checkout.
</Callout>

## Pay Later at Checkout

On **New Sale**, attach a **customer** first. Without a customer, **Pay later** stays disabled.

Click **Charge / Checkout**, then **Pay later** to create the order with nothing collected now. This works for **Pickup**, **Delivery**, **Dine-In**, and **In-Store** sales.

The success screen shows the order total and that it is **On credit**. The order appears in **Sales** with payment status **Partial** or **Unpaid** depending on whether anything was collected.

### Optional collection date

When you use **Pay later**, you can set an optional **promised collection date** on the order. This date is for your team’s reference; it does not create a separate scheduled payment row.

### Partial payment now, rest on credit

When the customer pays **part of the total** at checkout:

1. Lower the **amount to pay now** and choose a payment method for that portion.
2. Enable **Put the rest on customer credit**.
3. Optionally pick a **promised collection date** for the remainder.

The order is marked **On credit** for the unpaid balance. Collect the rest later from the order details page.

<Callout title="Note">
If **Customer Credit (Pay Later)** is off but you take a partial payment, you can still use **Save the rest as money owed** with a due date. That path creates a scheduled payment you track under [Overdue Payments](/docs/orders/overdue-payments). On-credit orders keep the balance on the order itself instead.
</Callout>

## Tracking What Customers Owe

### Customer profile

Open **Customers**, then a customer’s profile. When credit is enabled, the stats row includes **Amount owed**: the total still due on all open **On credit** orders for that customer.

If they owe money, use **View open credit orders** above their order history. It filters to deferred orders with an outstanding balance.

### Orders list

On **Sales > All Sales**:

| Filter | What it shows |
|--------|----------------|
| **Outstanding balance** | Orders that still have money due (including partial on-credit sales) |
| **Payment terms: On credit** | Orders intentionally sold on credit |

The payment column shows **On credit** under the payment status for deferred orders.

### Dashboard

Tiles that highlight orders needing attention link to the orders list with **Outstanding balance** applied so partial and on-credit sales are easy to find.

## Collecting Later

1. Open the order from **Sales** or the customer’s order history.
2. Confirm **Payment terms** shows **On credit** and review **Due date** if one was set.
3. Click **Add Payment** (or **Collect**) and enter the amount and method.

When the full balance is paid, the order moves to **Paid** and the customer’s **Amount owed** drops automatically. **Cancelled** on-credit orders no longer count toward amount owed.
