Store credit is a balance on the customer's account that can be used to pay for orders.

## How Balance Changes

- **Refund to store credit**: When you issue a [refund](/docs/returns-refunds/refunds) with the method **Store Credit**, the amount is added to the customer's balance.
- **Paying with store credit**: When taking payment on an order for this customer, enable **Use Store Credit** to apply the balance. If the credit does not cover the whole amount, the rest is paid with another method.

<Callout title="Note">
The balance shown on the customer page is managed automatically by orders and refunds. There is no manual "add credit" button. To give a customer credit, issue a refund with the Store Credit method.
</Callout>

## Paying With Store Credit in the Portal

Store credit appears as a payment option when a customer is selected and has a balance. See [Taking Payment and After the Sale](/docs/orders/pos-taking-payment) for the full checkout flow, including split payments when credit only covers part of the order.
