## What is Cashvio?

Cashvio is a free POS (cashier) and free online store for shops that sell in person, online, or both. One portal covers products, orders, customers, inventory, and reports. English and Arabic, with full RTL.

You get:

- A **portal** for daily work (sales, catalogue, stock, customers)
- An **online storefront** where shoppers browse, check out, and track orders
- **Multi-store** settings per location
- **Roles and permissions** so each teammate sees only what they need

## Who this guide is for

Business owners, store managers, and cashiers. No technical background required. Follow the path that matches where you are today.
