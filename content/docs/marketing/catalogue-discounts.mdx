Catalogue discounts mark items down on the shelf. Shoppers and cashiers see the sale price automatically. No one types a code. This is different from [coupons](/docs/marketing/coupons), where the customer or cashier enters a code at checkout.

## What a catalogue discount is

A catalogue discount reduces the unit price of matching items for a scheduled window. It applies at **New Sale**, on the **online storefront**, and in cart and checkout totals. Coupons still work on top after the markdown price is calculated.

## Create a catalogue discount

1. Open **Catalogue > Discounts** in the sidebar (requires the **Discounts** feature on your plan).
2. Click **Add discount**.
3. Set **Name**, **Store**, **Type** (percentage or fixed amount), and **Value**.
4. Choose **Scope**: variant, product, category, or whole store, then pick the target.
5. Set **Valid from** and **Valid to** dates.
6. Save. Status is derived from dates and the active toggle, not a separate scheduled job.

## When more than one rule matches

Only **one** catalogue discount wins per order line. Resolution order:

1. **Variant** (most specific)
2. **Product**
3. **Category**
4. **Store** (broadest)

If two rules share the same scope, the larger savings amount wins, then the newest rule.

## Coupons and minimum cart values

Catalogue markdowns apply first to the unit price. A valid coupon can still reduce the order afterward. **Minimum cart value** on a coupon is checked against the **discounted subtotal**, not the pre-sale list price.

Modifier add-ons are **not** discounted. Only the base variant price is marked down.

## Where shoppers and staff see it

| Surface | What appears |
| --- | --- |
| **POS product tiles** | Sale badge and struck-through original price |
| **POS ticket** | Line discount row and adjusted unit price |
| **Receipts** | Line-level discount detail |
| **Product lists (portal)** | Effective price and discount badge |
| **Online storefront** | Badge on cards, sale price on the product page, `/sale` listing |
| **Meta catalogue feed** | `sale_price` when a markdown is active |

## Reporting

Order **total discount** includes both coupon savings and catalogue markdowns. Staff analytics **discounts given** excludes automatic catalogue markdowns so scheduled sales are not blamed on individual cashiers. Your overall discount rate in reports may rise when you run more shelf sales. That is expected.

## Known limitation

On the online storefront, **sort by price** uses the pre-discount list price, not the effective sale price. Sale items still show the correct badge and checkout total. Use the **/sale** page or category filters to browse discounted products.
