---
title: Coupons & Discount Codes
description: "Overview of promotional coupons: percentage or fixed discounts, usage limits, and where to create, apply, and troubleshoot codes."
---

Coupons let you offer discount codes customers apply at checkout or on **New Sale**. You control the store, discount type, minimum cart value, which items qualify, usage limits, and validity dates.

**Key features:**
- Percentage or fixed-amount discounts
- Store-specific coupon management
- Minimum cart value requirements
- Whole-order discounts or targeting of specific products and categories
- Usage limits per coupon and per customer
- Date-based validity periods
- Real-time coupon validation
- Active/inactive status control
